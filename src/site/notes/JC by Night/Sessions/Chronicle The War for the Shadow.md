# Chronicle: The War for the Shadow

This chronicle is a three-act story of desperation, shifting alliances, and survival in the shadow of New York. The players (the Coterie) begin as loyal-but-disposable assets for a weak Prince, immediately mired in a turf war, only to discover a far deadlier threat is already inside the gates.

## Act I: The Turf War

**Theme:** The Rival You Know **Goal:** The Coterie must confront the Anarchs, establishing them as a primary rival and forcing the Coterie to handle a delicate political problem for their Prince.

### 1. The Hook (Session 1)

- **"The Pirate Radio Problem":** [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]] summons the Coterie to [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Durand Tower (Prince)\|Durand Tower (Prince)]], not about murders, but about _noise_. **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/DJ Trip (Toreador)\|DJ Trip (Toreador)]],"** the Anarchs' propagandist, has set up a new pirate radio broadcast powerful enough to be heard across the city. He's openly mocking the Prince, calling him a "Manhattan lapdog," and (most dangerously) dropping coded hints about Camarilla feeding grounds.
    
- **The Task:** Durand is furious and paranoid. He wants the broadcast _silenced_, and he wants the Coterie to deliver a "hard message" to **"Red" Valenti** to back off.
    
- **The Sheriff's "Help":** [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]] is present, looking unimpressed in her Carhartt jacket. "This is a pest, not a threat. My bat could handle this in an hour." Durand counters that a "bat" will start an open war he can't afford. He needs his "diplomats" (the Coterie) to handle it quietly. Petrova offers no resources and clearly expects them to fail, seeing them as the Prince's pampered pets.
    

### 2. The Investigation (Sessions 1-2)

- **Tracking the Signal:** The Coterie must find the broadcast's source. This is a street-level investigation. They might have to:
    
    - Bribe mortals (club kids, record store owners) who listen to the station.
        
    - Track Anarch "bagmen" like the twitchy **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Jinx (Malkavian)\|Jinx (Malkavian)]]"** moving equipment.
        
    - Use their own contacts, like the desperate [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]] at [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Gilded Cage VIP Room (Sal)\|The Gilded Cage]], to listen for rumors.
        
- **The "Daylight Corps":** The Coterie will find the Anarchs are one step ahead. Their "Daylight Corps" (Thin-Bloods) are moving equipment and acting as lookouts _during the day_, making the Anarch operation incredibly hard to pin down while the Coterie is forced to sleep.
    

### 3. The Confrontation (Session 3)

- **The "Studio":** The Coterie finally tracks the signal to its source: the "broadcast booth" in the basement of a condemned building in The Heights, defended by **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/DJ Trip (Toreador)\|DJ Trip (Toreador)]]"** and the Anarchs' pragmatic fixer, **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Stitch (Caitiff)\|Stitch (Caitiff)]]"**.
    
- **The "Boss Fight":** This is a social or physical confrontation. As they handle the lieutenants, the Anarchs' second-in-command, the feral **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Scrap (Gangrel)\|Scrap (Gangrel)]]"**, arrives with muscle. It's an ambush.
    
- **The "Diplomacy":** Before the fight escalates to Final Death, **"Red" Valenti** himself arrives, calling a halt. He's pragmatic. "So, you're the Prince's new attack dogs. You're a long way from the Waterfront. This isn't your turf. Why are we fighting over scraps when you've got bigger problems you don't even _see_?" He tells them to deliver _his_ message: "Stay out of The Heights, and maybe I'll tell my people to turn down the volume."
    

### 4. Fallout

- The Coterie has a choice: escalate the fight, or accept a tense "ceasefire." Either way, they've established a hot cold-war with the Anarchs. They've seen "Red's" operation and his "Daylight Corps" firsthand, realizing the Anarchs are a much more organized and dangerous threat than Durand believes.
    

## Act II: The Real Threat

**Theme:** The Boogeyman is Real **Goal:** The [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]] make their debut, forcing the Coterie to realize their Anarch problem is trivial and that they must seek help from an even more dangerous, unknown power.

### 1. The Hook (Session 4)

- **"The Front Page Problem":** Just as the Coterie is dealing with the Anarch fallout, _now_ the murders hit. The _Jersey Journal_ front page is a nightmare. Prince Durand _freaks out_. He immediately blames the Anarchs ("This is their chaos! This is 'Red' Valenti escalating!").
    
- **The Dissent:** Sheriff [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]] is grim. "This ain't Anarchs. This is sloppy, but it's not their style. This is something else." Durand ignores her and orders the Coterie to pin this on Valenti, or else.
    

### 2. The Investigation (Sessions 4-5)

- **The Scene:** The Coterie investigates the B-List hotel. The gruesome, flesh-crafted tableau is clearly not the Anarchs' work.
    
- **The Specialist:** They consult **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Regent Evelyn Reed\|Regent Evelyn Reed]]**, the city's Tremere Regent. She is professionally terrified, recognizing the work as a combination of Tzimisce flesh-crafting and Hecata necromancy—an uncontrolled supernatural event near the ley lines she is mapping. She reports _immediately_ to her Chantry, putting insane pressure on Durand. The Coterie now knows: **The [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]] is here.**
    

### 3. The Dead End (Session 6)

- **No Leads:** The trail goes cold. The [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]] pack ("The Gutter Saints") is professional and has vanished. The Anarchs are hostile, offering no help (they're just as scared and baffled). The Camarilla is useless.
    
- **The Desperate Idea:** Durand is in a full-blown panic, getting screamed at by Manhattan. "Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]], ever the spymaster, makes a quiet suggestion from his office in the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Montgomery Wellness Center (Doc Thorne)\|Montgomery Wellness Center (Doc Thorne)]]: "The only Kindred in a hundred miles who knows how to _fight_ Sabbat of that caliber... is an elder who has made a point of exterminating them from her domain. [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)]] in Hoboken _hates_ the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]]. Perhaps she can be... persuaded... to offer counsel."
    

### 4. The Diplomatic Mission (Session 7)

- **The Task:** Durand orders the Coterie to do the impossible: go to Hoboken, beg for an audience with **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)]]" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)]]**, and get _any_ information she has on this [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]] pack.
    
- **The Audience:** This is a high-stakes social "boss fight." The Coterie must navigate her terrifying [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)\|Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)]] and her bound lieutenants: the digital ghost **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Glitch (Nosferatu)\|Glitch (Nosferatu)]]"**, the volatile **Boro**, and the silent, imposing **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Ignacio Archado (Scourge)\|Ignacio Archado (Scourge)]]**.
    
- **The "Help":** [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)]] is _furious_. She is insulted that Durand's incompetence has allowed [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]] "filth" to operate so close to her perfect domain. She despises the Coterie. But, to protect her _own_ border, she gives them a single, cryptic clue:
    
    - _"My degenerate kin do not hide in hotels. They build cathedrals of flesh and iron. They nest in the places mortals have abandoned. Look for the _rust_, where the city's bones are exposed."_
        
- **Fallout:** The Coterie has a real lead. But they have also, by asking for help, placed themselves in [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)]]'s "debt." She _will_ call on them later.
    

## Act III: The Siege

**Theme:** The Choice **Goal:** The Coterie uses [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)]]'s intel to find the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]], only to uncover their master plan, forcing a final choice of allegiance that will decide the fate of Jersey City.

### 1. The Hunt (Session 8)

- **The Discovery:** Following [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)]]'s clue ("rust," "city's bones"), the Coterie investigates the industrial yards and the abandoned rail lines, eventually finding the **meatpacking plant**—a "cathedral of rust and iron."
    
- **The Boss Fight:** The Coterie assaults the haven, facing **"The Gutter Saints"** (Zantosa, Bianca, and Brother Anansi). This is a brutal fight.
    

### 2. The Master Plan (Session 9)

- **The Aftermath:** The pack is destroyed, but in their haven, the Coterie finds the _real_ prize: maps of the old rail tunnels leading to the **Holland Tunnel**, surveillance on Manhattan, and ritual notes from **Bishop Kovic**.
    
- **The Truth:** "The Gutter Saints" were a distraction. The _real_ war is _underground_. Kovic is planning to move a [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]] invasion force _through_ Jersey City for a surprise, decapitation strike on Manhattan.
    

### 3. The Internal Rot (Session 10)

- **The Sheriff's Move:** **Sheriff [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]]** is stunned. The Coterie _succeeded_ where she would have failed. They found the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]] _and_ made contact with [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)]]. She now sees them as _real_ players and a _real_ threat to her own power.
    
- **The Setup:** She begins to undermine them. She "leaks" info about one of their havens to the Anarchs (to "test" their rivalry) or demands they "report" all findings from the Autarkis to _her_ at [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Petrova's Garage\|Petrova's Garage]], not the Prince. The Coterie must now navigate an internal war as the external one is about to explode.
    

### 4. The City at War (Session 11)

- **The Siege Begins:** Kovic learns his pack is gone and his plan is exposed. He launches the invasion _now_. He sends [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]] packs on suicide missions all over the city—against the Camarilla's Waterfront, the Anarchs' Docks, and the Autarkis's border—to create maximum chaos.
    
- **The Factions Scramble:**
    
    - **Camarilla:** Durand and his loyalists lock themselves in [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Durand Tower (Prince)\|Durand Tower (Prince)]], totally useless. Sheriff [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]] is on the streets, fighting a losing, defensive war.
        
    - **Anarchs:** "Red" Valenti and "[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Scrap (Gangrel)\|Scrap (Gangrel)]]" are in a desperate fight to hold their territory, taking heavy losses.
        
    - **Autarkis:** Hoboken goes into total lockdown. [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)]]'s borders are sealed, and she kills _anything_ that crosses the line.
        
- **The Coterie's Position:** The Coterie is the only group with the knowledge and power to stop the _real_ threat: Kovic himself, who is commanding the invasion from his "Cathedral of Rust" beneath the Pulaski Skyway.
    

### 5. The Choice (Session 12)

- The Coterie must fight their way to the Pulaski Skyway to stop Kovic. Who they are fighting _for_ is their final choice.
    

1. **Loyalty (Camarilla):** They fight in the name of the Prince. They assault Kovic's fortress, stop the invasion, and save the city for the Camarilla. They become the _de facto_ power in a city with a grateful (and still weak) Prince, but they are forever tied to their Manhattan masters.
    
2. **Alliance (Anarchs):** They go to "Red" Valenti first, who is under siege. They offer a true alliance: "We know Kovic's plan. Help us stop him, and we'll decide who runs this city _after_." They lead a combined Coterie-Anarch force to stop Kovic, with the goal of "removing" Durand and [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]] in the chaos and claiming the city for a new, pragmatic "brotherhood."
    
3. **Treason ([[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]]):** They see the writing on the wall. They go to the Pulaski Skyway not to fight, but to _join_. They offer Bishop Kovic the head of Prince Durand (or [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)]]) as a sign of their loyalty. They help the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]] win, becoming the new, monstrous lords of the city.
    
4. **Independence (Autarkis):** They abandon the city. They flee to Hoboken and offer [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)]] their "debt": Kovic's head and his battle plans. They lead _her_ forces (Boro and [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Ignacio Archado (Scourge)\|Ignacio Archado (Scourge)]]) in a surgical strike, not to save Jersey City, but to _protect Hoboken_. In return, they gain amnesty and sanctuary in her domain, watching their old city burn from across the border.
    

### 6. Finale

- The campaign culminates in a final, desperate confrontation with **Bishop Kovic** and his elite guard in his haven beneath the highway, as the sounds of the invasion force massing in the tunnels echo all around them. The fate of Jersey City—and the first shot in the war for New York—is in their hands.
