# Lucien Graal

**Vibe:** "The polite phantom who catalogues the abyss, hoping to find a soul within it."

*   **Clan:** Tremere
*   **Concept:** Reluctant Occult Investigator & Academic Exile

---

### **Appearance**

Lucien's appearance is likely as meticulous and understated as his research. He carries the quiet, observant air of an academic, a man more comfortable in libraries and labs than in the violent streets of Jersey City. He is a polite, unassuming phantom, easily overlooked until his sharp intellect is required.

### **Vitals & Personality**

Disciplined and clinical, Lucien moves through the night as a quiet technician of the unseen. He studies the city's supernatural rot with precision, searching for patterns in blood and structure in chaos. The Embrace, however, ignited a curiosity that borders on uncontrollable, pushing him to explore the possibilities of damnation. He is caught in a state of perpetual conflict: too Tremere to be trusted by outsiders, yet too human to be truly loyal to the cold machinations of his clan.

### **Background**

In his mortal life, Lucien Graal was an adjunct professor at Rutgers–Newark, specializing in "symbolic necrology"—the study of how humanity has historically tried to communicate with the dead. This academic devotion attracted the attention of **Evelyn Reed**, a visiting scholar and Tremere who saw his sharp mind as a tool to be forged. The Embrace was a deliberate test, but the transformation made Lucien more inquisitive and less compliant than she had intended.

Now, Lucien finds himself in a quiet exile in Jersey City, officially serving as part of the Prince's "cleanup" coterie. His role is to investigate occult messes, analyze blood anomalies, and erase supernatural evidence before it can breach the Masquerade.

### **Ambition & Motivation**

Lucien is driven by a desperate need to understand his own existence. He studies the city's chaos hoping to find a pattern that will explain why his sire, Evelyn, created him—and, more importantly, why she hasn't yet destroyed him. Beneath this existential dread is a deeply ingrained, perhaps futile, desire to finally prove himself worthy of her time.

### **Haven & Herd**

Lucien operates from a decaying brownstone near the Waterfront, a haven that smells of iron, old books, and incense. He has no formal herd, reflecting his solitary and clinical nature.

### **Relationships & Touchstones**

**Sire: [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Regent Evelyn Reed\|Regent Evelyn Reed]]**
*   The Prince's official Tremere consultant and Lucien's sire. Evelyn views Lucien as a failed experiment and a "loose end" she has yet to quietly cut. She does not acknowledge their bond in public, and her private interactions with him are cold and clinical, treating him like a specimen to be catalogued. Lucien, **bound by blood** and a desperate need for approval, has never learned how to disobey her.
* Secretly Sired

**Touchstone: Dr. Elise Navarro (Former Colleague & Friend)**
*   Once Lucien's closest research partner, Elise represented rational compassion and grounded his academic pursuits in humanity. She believes he succumbed to the cultic fringes of academia and now teaches his unfinished work, preserving the memory of a man who no longer exists. Lucien secretly funds her research and leaves annotated books for her, clinging to her as the last living proof that he once sought knowledge for love, not power.

**Touchstone: Mark Ellison (Former Student)**
*   A brilliant and idealistic former student who saw Lucien as a moral compass. Mark has preserved Lucien's old research, refusing to believe his mentor simply vanished. Lucien sometimes watches over him, unseen, viewing Mark as a symbol of integrity—a reminder that knowledge should serve to help others, not just to gain power.

**(Conviction: Understanding must serve compassion.)**
*   Lucien believes knowledge is sacred only if it heals, clarifies, or protects. Using his intellect or blood sorcery to manipulate, harm, or dominate causes a sharp erosion of his self, haunted by the certainty that Elise and Mark would see nothing human left in him.
