# Rhiannon Wright

**Vibe:** "I just don’t know which lie hurts more — that I can save them, or that I can’t."

*   **Clan:** Salubri (Secret)
*   **Generation:** 12th
*   **Apparent Age:** Mid-20s
*   **Concept:** Wounded Healer & Moral Compass

---

### **Appearance**

Rhiannon is striking in a quiet, impossible way—beautiful but unassuming, as though her presence is too gentle for the world she inhabits. Her copper-ginger hair catches the light like fading fire, often tied loosely at her neck to keep her face clear while she works. Her eyes are large and expressive, carrying a profound exhaustion that belies her youthful appearance. She holds herself with a fragile grace, as though she might break, yet never does. Beneath the fringe of her hair, she keeps a small bandage to conceal the third eye that is the hidden seal of her clan.

### **Vitals & Personality**

Driven by a deep, almost painful empathy, Rhiannon serves as the court's moral center and mediator. She is the one who still believes in mercy long after others have abandoned it. Her kindness is not a weakness but a profound, unwavering conviction. However, this compassion comes at a great personal cost, leaving her perpetually caught between the brutal necessities of Kindred politics and her own code. She is the quiet, tragic figure who heals others while slowly bleeding herself dry.

### **Background**

In her mortal life, Rhiannon was a night-shift nurse at an underfunded city clinic, where she treated the city's forgotten and marginalized. Her boundless compassion and the exhaustion it bred drew the attention of her Sire. He appeared to her one night during a storm, a dying man whom she tried to save. He offered her the power to "never lose another patient again," and when she awoke from the Embrace, he was gone. His only legacy was the Thirst, her new abilities, and his final words: "Heal them. Even if they hate you for it."

### **Ambition & Motivation**

Rhiannon's ambition is not for power, but for peace. She is desperately trying to hold the Prince's fractured court together, believing that if she can just save him from his own darkness, she can save the entire city from the chaos that threatens to consume it. She is motivated by a profound, perhaps naive, belief that redemption is possible, even for the damned.

### **Haven & Herd**

Rhiannon maintains a small, spartan apartment near the clinic where she once worked. It is clean, quiet, and devoid of personal effects, more a place to rest than a home. She continues to work at the clinic under an assumed name, and its patients—the addicts, the unhoused, the desperate—form her herd. She feeds from them with their consent, often in moments when she is using her powers to ease their pain, fulfilling her role as a true consensualist.

### **Relationships & Touchstones**

**Touchstone: The Old Man Under the Bridge**
*   An elderly, unhoused man with dementia whom Rhiannon cared for in life and continues to watch over in unlife. She brings him food and medicine, a secret act of charity. He represents the unwavering mercy she clings to.
*   **(Conviction: You must heal the wounds of the world, no matter the cost to yourself.)**

**[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]] (Blood Bond)**
*   The Prince treats Rhiannon as both a beautiful possession and a fragile confidant. In public, she is a symbol of his ability to command peace; in private, he shows her a rare gentleness. She is bound to his will, unable to betray him, and this complex dynamic fuels her hope that she can be the one to save him from himself.

**Lucien (Antagonist/Rival)**
*   A courtier who sees Rhiannon's mercy not as a virtue but as a lie she tells herself. He is a cynical voice of truth that constantly challenges her worldview, suggesting her suffering is self-inflicted.

**Sire (Unknown)**
*   A mysterious figure, believed to be an Enlightened Salubri, who saw in Rhiannon a vessel for his philosophy. His parting words are the core of her mission and her curse.
