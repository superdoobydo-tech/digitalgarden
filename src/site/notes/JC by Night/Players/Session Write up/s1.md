Session 1

  

It is a crisp autumn night in New Jersey. The coterie readies themselves to meet with the Prince. Mia wakes up in her shitty traphouse, and dresses in the nicest clothes she owns (not super nice). She walks to the bus stop, and runs into Mark, her ghoul, on the way. He asks for some blood, and suggests giving her a ride downtown in exchange for blood. She declines, promising she’ll get back to him tonight and let him have blood. She hops on the bus towards Duran Tower to meet the Prince.

  

Grayson Walker picks up Rhiannon from her apartment, driving a blacked out SUV. Grayson is taking his bodyguard job very seriously.

  

Kazimir makes his way to the prince in his car, wearing a nice suit.

  

Lucian is at home, sitting in a blood circle with runes. He realizes he’s late and freaks out, jumping up and quickly hiding his blood circle.

  

Kazimir and Lucien arrive at the tower, and take the elevator to the penthouse. They exchange some banter, each thinking they are better than the other.

  

The other three arrive, and Rhiannon and Mia hug, catching up. Mia meets Grayson, and she explains she’s a huge fan, and flirts with him.

  

Upon arriving at the penthouse, the coterie are offered blood refreshments, and Rhiannon shows kindness to the masked server Harold as he’s serving blood. Because Lucein is a fucking weirdo, he bows to Rhiannon, reminding her about their next delivery. She agrees, and is clearly pretty tired of dealing with Lucien.

  

Lucian's sire Evelyn Reed, the local tremere primogen who runs the local chantry is there as well. They walk into the main chambers of the Prince. Prince Marcel Duran chastises Rhiannon for entering without knocking, but lets it slide because he likes her. They exchange smiles. Sheriff Nails is also present with the Prince, wearing overalls and flannel. Mia scowls at Sheriff Nails, and she scowls back. The Malkavian owner of the local wellness clinic is also present. The coterie sits down at the table with Prince Marcel Duran.



The Prince says the Anarchs have taken some territory from the Camarilla, and explains he needs the coteries help. A radio stops playing Smooth Jazz Music, and switches to a local DJ. The DJ playfully insults and trash talks The Prince with immature humor. The Prince is extremely mad at the Anarchs on the radio station making fun of him, raving and calling for them to be killed. Evelan suggests he sends the coterie to “check out what’s happening on the north side” instead. He refuses, insisting the coterie kills DJ Grip. He says these people intentionally cut into his favorite station. Kazimir chuckles at this, finding this whole situation absurd. The Prince tries to compel him, but Kazimir resists it. As they leave, they overhear that there are disappearances and mystery in the north.

  

Mia plans to speak to members in her community (using her underground fame) Grayson, Rhiannon and Lucien make their way to a local club to seek out information (though Lucien takes the bus) and Kazimir plans to scope out online forums for any mention of DJ Grip and where he might be broadcasting from.
