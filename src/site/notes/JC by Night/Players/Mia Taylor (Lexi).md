### **Appearance**

Mia is a disheveled figure with short, greasy light brown hair, dark blue eyes, and persistent dark circles from a life lived hard. Her face is adorned with numerous piercings, and her body is a canvas of amateur, colorful tattoos, including emojis, mushrooms, names, and a few cruder designs. While not conventionally beautiful, she often dresses to be seen as "hot," favoring revealing crop tops and fishnets. When not performing, her attire defaults to practical hoodies and jeans.

### **Vitals & Personality**

Charismatic and vibrant, Mia comes alive in social situations. She lives for the thrill of the moment, chasing the euphoria of the party scene. A former cocaine addict in life, she continues this pattern in unlife by seeking out and feeding from mortals under the influence. Her true happy place is behind a mixing table at an illicit rave, surrounded by the euphoric art of lights, music, and the rhythmic bass that she considers the pinnacle of experience. She also enjoys graffiti and has a history of conflict with local skinheads.

### **Background**

Raised in poverty, Mia's life was shaped by hardship. Her father was incarcerated for murder when she was young, and her mother was neglectful until her death when Mia was 20. After dropping out of high school, Mia spent what little money she had on parties, drugs, and fleeting relationships.

She was Embraced by a fellow Toreador, Eliza Matthew, who saw the raw, chaotic beauty in her DJing at a rave. Eliza offered Mia a way out of the slums, but Mia refused, clinging to her community. Seeing her as a lost cause unwilling to leave her squalor, Eliza departed, leaving Mia with a lingering sense of regret.

### **Ambition & Motivation**

Mia dreams of becoming a world-famous DJ, using her success to lift herself and her community out of poverty. However, her hedonistic lifestyle, ongoing addiction, and general immaturity constantly sabotage this aspiration. Desperate for cash to support her people and her habits, she has taken on work for the Prince, leveraging her intimate knowledge of the Jersey City streets.

### **Haven & Herd**

Mia’s primary haven is a traphouse in a bad neighborhood, likely condemned. She shares this space with a rotating cast of about half a dozen people, including her small herd of groupies and fellow addicts, and her ghoul, Mark. The constant presence of people nodding off ensures her day sleep is rarely disturbed. When not at home, she is no stranger to couch-surfing or even sleeping in the sewers, a holdover from her mortal life.

### **Relationships & Touchstones**

**Touchstone: Daniel Taylor (Father)**
*   Daniel has been in prison for murder since Mia was a child. She is convinced he is innocent and that a corrupt cop framed him. Her goal is to one day earn enough money to either hire a top lawyer or break him out herself.
*   **(Conviction: Authority is corrupt and cannot be trusted.)**

**Touchstone: Beth Johnson (Lifelong Friend)**
*   Beth is a fellow addict and sex worker who once saved Mia's life after a fentanyl overdose. She is a core part of Mia's community, even if she doesn't live in the traphouse.
*   **(Conviction: You must take care of your community.)**

**Retainer/Ghoul: Mark Walker**
*   Mark lives with Mia and has been a long-term, on-and-off situationship. Tired of the mixed signals and emotional complexity, Mia ghouled him to "simplify" the relationship into one of loyalty.

**Sire: Eliza Matthew**
*   Eliza saw the artist in Mia but abandoned her as a lost cause when Mia refused to leave her life behind. The relationship is one of regret and disappointment.

**Friend: (Lyla's Character)**
