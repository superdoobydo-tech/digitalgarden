Disciplined and tough from his wrestling background, Grayson is pragmatic and serious. He minds his own business as long as he's getting paid and isn't picky about work. He still clings to his wrestling character, referring to himself as "the champ."

In his mortal life, Grayson grew up admiring tough guy heel characters in wrestling. However, his sire orchestrated a deliberate fall from grace, ruining his career before embracing him when he had nothing left.

### **Ambition & Motivation**

Grayson is driven by the need to earn and maintain his existence as a paid bodyguard. He continues to embody his "champ" persona, perhaps subconsciously seeking to prove himself again, even without understanding the true cause of his downfall.

Grayson's haven is **The Warehouse**, a medium-sized warehouse with an open area and stairs leading up to a domicile where offices would normally be. He hasn't had much time to unpack.
