### **"Glitch" (Nosferatu) - The Digital Spider**

#### **Appearance**
Glitch is a pale, skeletal figure, almost always hunched over a bank of glowing CRT monitors. Their face is typically lost in the deep shadow of their hoodie and the harsh, ever-present glare of the screens, which cast an eerie, shifting light on their gaunt features. Their haven carries the distinct, pervasive scent of ozone, dust, and stale, recycled air, a reflection of their ceaseless digital existence.

#### **Role and Demeanor**
**[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]]'s Digital Spymaster.** Glitch is a '90s-era phreaker and hacker, a ghost in the machine before the machine was truly built. They are [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]]'s primary source of modern surveillance and information, acting as her technological anchor in Hoboken. They relentlessly tap phone lines, hack the primitive servers of local businesses, and constantly monitor police and emergency frequencies. Glitch serves as [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]]'s early warning system and information broker, a perfect, technologically advanced counterpoint to her old-world methods of control and the physical surveillance provided by the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)\|Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)]].

#### **Motivation: Digital Sovereignty & Unseen Power**
Glitch is driven by the thrill of the digital hunt and the profound satisfaction of uncovering secrets that others believe are hidden. They thrive in the shadows of the nascent internet, finding a sense of unparalleled power and purpose in manipulating the unseen currents of information. Their loyalty to [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] is pragmatic and absolute; she provides the resources, the targets, and the undisturbed sanctuary necessary for their work. In return, Glitch delivers the crucial intelligence that keeps Hoboken safe from outside threats, directly supporting the Countess's motivation for **Sovereignty** and maintaining the pristine order of her domain.

#### **Relationships**
*   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]]**: Lieutenant and digital spymaster for the Countess. Their loyalty is pragmatic and absolute, as she provides the resources and sanctuary Glitch needs.
*   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]] (Camarilla)**: Glitch views Durand as a relic, an old-world ruler whose obsession with appearances and physical control makes him digitally vulnerable. He's a predictable source of data, easily circumvented by Glitch's skills.
*   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]] (Camarilla)**: Petrova represents crude, physical enforcement. Glitch has a grudging respect for his street-level effectiveness but finds his methods archaic and easily detectable through digital means. He's a physical threat, but not one Glitch can't see coming in the digital realm.
*   **"Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]] (Camarilla)**: Thorne is a fellow information gatherer, albeit through psychological means. Glitch finds him an interesting, if analog, counterpart. There might be a strange intellectual kinship, as both deal in hidden truths, and Glitch might even consider trading digital data for Thorne's unique psychological insights.
*   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]] (Camarilla)**: Sal's desperation to revive [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Gilded Cage VIP Room (Sal)\|The Gilded Cage]] and cling to relevance makes him a goldmine of easily intercepted gossip and financial vulnerabilities. Glitch sees him as a low-hanging fruit for data extraction and a source of amusingly transparent schemes.
*   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Regent Evelyn Reed\|Regent Evelyn Reed]] (Camarilla)**: Evelyn's geomantic engineering and arcane pursuits are outside Glitch's digital domain. They would likely dismiss her methods as archaic but acknowledge the raw power she seeks. Glitch might see her as a potential rival in the pursuit of hidden power, or perhaps a source of unique, non-digital data that could be digitized or exploited for the Countess's benefit.

#### **Haven: The Digital Cocoon**
Glitch's haven is a blacked-out apartment within a non-descript building near the Stevens Institute of Technology in Hoboken, known as [[Berg Apartments Glitch Haven\|Berg Apartments Glitch Haven]]. It is less a physical dwelling and more a digital nerve center, a sanctuary built from the detritus of the burgeoning digital age.
-   **The Interior:** Stepping into Glitch's apartment is like entering a perpetual digital night. The windows are completely covered, blocking out all natural light and plunging the space into an artificial twilight. The air is thick with the scent of ozone, dust, and the faint, metallic tang of overworked electronics. Every available surface is a chaotic mess: stolen computer parts, circuit boards, and discarded wires are strewn across desks, piled on the floor, and even hanging from makeshift hooks. A labyrinth of tangled phone lines, Ethernet cables, and power cords snakes across the floor and up the walls, connecting a sprawling network of whirring hard drives and humming servers. The only illumination comes from the hypnotic, flickering glow of numerous CRT monitors, casting an eerie, ever-shifting light on Glitch. Half-eaten takeout containers and empty energy drink cans litter the few clear spots.
-   **Security & Resources:** The haven is secured primarily through its utter lack of physical presence and Glitch's deep integration into the digital infrastructure. Its anonymity ensures it remains unseen and unheard by casual observers. While physical defenses might be minimal, the true security lies in Glitch's mastery of the digital world, making it virtually impenetrable to digital intrusion and providing an advanced early warning system for any threats. The haven itself is Glitch's greatest resource—a vast, constantly updated network of stolen and intercepted information.
