### **"The Countess" Alexia Vros (Toreador) - The Solitary Sovereign**

#### **Appearance**
The Countess is timelessly elegant, with the unmistakable bearing of old European aristocracy. She favors bespoke, minimalist '90s haute couture—think Jil Sander or Helmut Lang—which contrasts sharply with her unnervingly perfect, sculpted features. Her posture is always impeccable, her movements fluid and deliberate, exuding a serene control that is both captivating and utterly terrifying. She is a living work of art, meticulously curated, and expects nothing less from her surroundings.

#### **Role and Demeanor**
**The Solitary Ruler of Hoboken.** The Countess is an elder Toreador, but she holds no loyalty to the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Camarilla (JC)\|Camarilla (JC)]]. She is an Autarkis, an artist and a *curator* who sees Hoboken as her personal, perfect salon. The mortals, the architecture, the very *atmosphere* of the city are all part of her meticulously maintained collection. She enforces the Masquerade with vicious efficiency, not out of adherence to Camarilla law, but because "one does not permit flaws in the gallery." She desires nothing more than to be left alone to her "work," maintaining the pristine beauty and order of her domain. Her tolerance for any outside interference, particularly from figures like [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Russo]] (Durand) or the chaotic [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]], is paper-thin. She is a force of quiet, unyielding will, and her displeasure is a cold, precise instrument.

#### **Motivation**
The Countess's core motivation is **Sovereignty**. She has painstakingly built her perfect, quiet domain in Hoboken, and she will burn anyone—be they [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Camarilla (JC)\|Camarilla (JC)]], [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]], or [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Anarchs (JC)\|Anarch]]—to the ground who threatens its stability. She harbors no grander ambitions for power or expansion, but she will never, under any circumstances, relinquish what she has created and claimed as her own. Her rule is absolute within her borders, and she demands the same unwavering devotion from her lieutenants as she gives to her vision of Hoboken.

#### **Relationships with Her Court & Other Factions**
The Countess generally despises the company of other Kindred, seeing them as rivals or potential disruptions. She has made rare exceptions, binding her lieutenants to her will through the Vinculum (Blood Bond) to act as extensions of her sovereignty.

-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Glitch (Nosferatu)\|Glitch (Nosferatu)]] (Digital Spymaster):** She relies on Glitch as her technological anchor, valuing their ability to provide modern surveillance and information. She sees Glitch as a necessary, if aesthetically unrefined, tool for maintaining her domain's security in the digital age.
-   **Boro (Tremere) (Mad Dog):** Boro is her volatile weapon, a master of Thaumaturgy kept on a tight leash. She unleashes him on threats that need to be _annihilated_, valuing his destructive rage when precision is not required.
-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Ignacio Archado (Scourge)\|Ignacio Archado (Scourge)]] (Silent Blade):** Archado is her personal Scourge, a former [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]] fanatic whose loyalty was earned when she diablorized his sire. She respects his unyielding conviction and uses him as the merciless enforcer of her interpretation of the Traditions within Hoboken.
-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)\|Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)]] (Eyes and Ears):** Her network of ghouls, like [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)\|Mrs. Agnello]] and [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)\|Sully]], are her eyes and ears on the ground. She values their seamless integration into mortal society and their efficiency in handling minor threats, ensuring her domain remains undisturbed.
-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]] (Camarilla):** She views Durand as an irritating political headache, a weak ruler whose anemic coterie and the chaos of Jersey City constantly threaten her borders. She tolerates his existence only because New York's Camarilla is unwilling to dislodge her.
-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]]**: Her hatred for the Sabbat's chaos is absolute. Any attempt by them to use Hoboken as a staging ground is met with lethal force.

#### **Haven: [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/Locations/Gallery Brownstone - Countess Alexia Vros\|Gallery Brownstone - Countess Alexia Vros]]**
Her haven encompasses the entire top two floors of a meticulously restored brownstone on Castle Point Terrace. It is a fortress of beauty and silence, a physical manifestation of her curated domain.
-   **The Salon (First Floor):** An immaculate, old-world salon designed for receiving guests. Every piece of furniture, every painting, every antique is perfectly placed, creating an atmosphere of refined elegance that is both inviting and subtly intimidating. It is a space where she can observe and assess visitors within her carefully controlled environment.
-   **The Private Gallery (Top Floor):** Her private sanctuary, an immaculate gallery filled with priceless, stolen, and forbidden art. Here, she obsesses over her collection, finding solace and inspiration in the beauty she has acquired.
-   **Security:** Her haven is protected by a state-of-the-art security system, seamlessly integrated into the historic architecture. More importantly, it is guarded by her loyal [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)\|Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)]]. Some whisper that her most favored ghouls are so perfectly still and beautiful, they are often mistaken for statues... until they move.
