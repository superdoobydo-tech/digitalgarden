# Faction Briefing: The Hoboken Autarkis

**Vibe:** "This is my town. Stay in yours. I am not asking."

### Overview: The Thorn in the Side

Hoboken is a one-vampire state, an anomaly that shouldn't exist. Sitting directly on Manhattan's flank, this small, mile-square city is ruled by a single, powerful Autarkis (an independent) who refuses to bow to the Camarilla. This is "[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]]" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|Alexia Vros]].

Her independence is a constant, screaming political headache for [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Russo]] (Durand). The Camarilla of New York detests having an unknown, powerful Kindred—especially a Toreador elder of her stature—rejecting her clan and the Camarilla to rule a domain so close to their own. They pressure Russo weekly to "handle" her, but they know she is too powerful for his anemic coterie to dislodge, and they are unwilling to spare the resources themselves. For now, she is a wild card in the war, and every faction wants to know which way she'll jump.

### Core Territory: The Mile-Square City

[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] claims all of Hoboken as her domain. In the 90s, it's a city in transition, caught between its old-world Italian-American roots and a new influx of yuppies and artists looking for cheaper rent with a view of the skyline.

-   **Washington Street:** Her main "hunting ground." The main thoroughfare of bars, old-world delis, and new bistros provides a dense, varied herd, which she "curates."
-   **The Brownstones:** She considers the residents of the historic brownstones her "flock," and she patrols these quiet, tree-lined streets personally, ensuring no other Kindred hunts here without her express permission.
-   **The Waterfront:** She fiercely guards her border along the Hudson. Any [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]] or Camarilla attempting to use her docks as an entry point into the city (or as a launchpad to NY) are met with lethal force.

### Key Figure

1.  **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]]" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|Alexia Vros]] (Toreador):**
    -   **Look:** Timelessly elegant, with the unmistakable bearing of old European aristocracy. She favors bespoke, minimalist '90s haute couture—think Jil Sander or Helmut Lang—which contrasts sharply with her unnervingly perfect, sculpted features. Her posture is always impeccable, her movements fluid and deliberate, exuding a serene control that is both captivating and utterly terrifying. She is a living work of art, meticulously curated, and expects nothing less from her surroundings.
    -   **Role:** The solitary ruler of Hoboken. [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] is an elder Toreador, but she holds no loyalty to the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Camarilla (JC)\|Camarilla (JC)]]. She is an artist and a _curator_ who sees Hoboken as her personal, perfect salon. The mortals, the architecture, the very _atmosphere_ are all part of her collection. She enforces the Masquerade _viciously_, not out of Camarilla loyalty, but because "one does not permit flaws in the gallery." She desires nothing more than to be left alone to her "work," maintaining the pristine beauty and order of her domain. Her tolerance for any outside interference, particularly from figures like [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Russo]] (Durand) or the chaotic [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]], is paper-thin. She is a force of quiet, unyielding will, and her displeasure is a cold, precise instrument.
    -   **Motivation:** **Sovereignty.** She has painstakingly built her perfect, quiet domain in Hoboken, and she will burn anyone—be they [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Camarilla (JC)\|Camarilla (JC)]], [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]], or [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Anarchs (JC)\|Anarch]]—to the ground who threatens its stability. She harbors no grander ambitions for power or expansion, but she will never, under any circumstances, relinquish what she has created and claimed as her own. Her rule is absolute within her borders, and she demands the same unwavering devotion from her lieutenants as she gives to her vision of Hoboken.
    -   **Haven:** The entire top two floors of a meticulously restored brownstone on Castle Point Terrace, known as [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/Locations/Gallery Brownstone - Countess Alexia Vros\|Gallery Brownstone - Countess Alexia Vros]]. The first floor is an immaculate, old-world salon for receiving guests. The top floor is an immaculate, private gallery of priceless, stolen, and forbidden art, where she obsesses over her collection. Her haven is protected by Ghouls and a state-of-the-art security system. Some whisper that her most favored ghouls are so perfectly still and beautiful they are mistaken for statues... until they move.

### Key Lieutenants

[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] generally despises the company of other Kindred, seeing them as rivals or potential disruptions. She has made rare exceptions, binding her lieutenants to her will through the Vinculum (Blood Bond) to act as extensions of her sovereignty.

1.  **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Glitch (Nosferatu)\|Glitch]]" (Nosferatu):**
    -   **Look:** A pale, skeletal figure, almost always hunched over a bank of glowing CRT monitors. Their face is typically lost in the deep shadow of their hoodie and the harsh, ever-present glare of the screens, which cast an eerie, shifting light on their gaunt features. Their haven carries the distinct, pervasive scent of ozone, dust, and stale, recycled air, a reflection of their ceaseless digital existence.
    -   **Role:** [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]]'s digital spymaster. "[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Glitch (Nosferatu)\|Glitch]]" is a '90s-era phreaker and hacker, a ghost in the machine before the machine was truly built. They run her modern surveillance, tapping phone lines, hacking the primitive servers of local businesses, and monitoring police and emergency frequencies. They are her early warning system and information broker, a perfect, technologically advanced counterpoint to her old-world methods of control and the physical surveillance provided by the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)\|Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)]].
    -   **Motivation:** Driven by the thrill of the digital hunt and the profound satisfaction of uncovering secrets that others believe are hidden. They thrive in the shadows of the nascent internet, finding a sense of unparalleled power and purpose in manipulating the unseen currents of information. Their loyalty to [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] is pragmatic and absolute; she provides the resources, the targets, and the undisturbed sanctuary necessary for their work, directly supporting the Countess's motivation for **Sovereignty** and maintaining the pristine order of her domain.
    -   **Haven:** A blacked-out apartment within a non-descript building near the Stevens Institute of Technology in Hoboken, known as [[Berg Apartments Glitch Haven\|Berg Apartments Glitch Haven]]. It is less a physical dwelling and more a digital nerve center, a sanctuary built from the detritus of the burgeoning digital age.

2.  **Boro (Tremere):**
    -   **Look:** A tall man with a raw, scarred face and burn marks visible on his hands and neck. He looks like a barely-contained explosion, dressed in practical, scorched work clothes. His eyes are intense and furious.
    -   **Role:** [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]]'s "Mad Dog." Boro is a Tremere who either fled or was exiled from his Chantry. He is a master of Thaumaturgy, but a volatile and unstable one. [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] keeps him on a tight leash via the Blood Bond. He is not a scalpel; he's a bomb. She unleashes him on threats that need to be _annihilated_, valuing his destructive rage when precision is not required.
    -   **Haven:** The abandoned boiler room of a defunct textile mill. It's hot, oppressive, and filled with the charred remains of his... "experiments."

3.  **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Ignacio Archado (Scourge)\|Ignacio Archado]] (Lasombra) - The Silent Inquisitor of Hoboken:**
    -   **Look:** Archado is a figure of stark, severe antiquity, a shadow given form. He wears no '90s fashion, opting instead for timeless, simple black clothing of a high-quality, matte fabric—a high-collared tunic and trousers that suggest a modern interpretation of clerical or monastic garb. He is unnaturally still, a statue carved from shadow, and his movements are economical and precise, each gesture carrying a deliberate weight. His face is gaunt, framed by short, severe black hair, but his presence is dominated by his eyes. His gaze is not just piercing; it is *heavy*, carrying the weight of centuries of judgment and an unyielding, almost fatalistic resolve. He is a man of few words, but his very presence speaks volumes of a dangerous, unshakeable conviction.
    -   **Role:** [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]]'s personal Scourge, the silent, merciless blade that enforces her will within Hoboken. He is the Kindred responsible for hunting and destroying the unproven, the unwanted, and the unsanctioned within Hoboken's meticulously guarded borders. He is the final, bloody word on the Tradition of Progeny *as interpreted by the Countess*. He sees unauthorized embraces or any external interference in Hoboken as a spiritual stain, a blasphemy against the pristine order of her domain, and he performs his duties with the cold, dispassionate zeal of an inquisitor. His actions are swift, brutal, and leave no room for argument or appeal.
    -   **Motivation:** Archado's core drive is absolute order and the preservation of [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]]'s domain, a conviction born from a profound, almost religious fanaticism. A former member of the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]], Archado witnessed [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] diablorize his sire, a brutal display of raw power and dominance that shattered his previous loyalties and earned his absolute, unyielding respect. Recognizing her strength, he transferred his fanatical devotion entirely to her and the absolute sovereignty of Hoboken. He is loyal not to any Prince or sect, but to the sacred letter of the Traditions *as they apply to the Countess's domain*.
    -   **Haven:** The cold, stone undercroft of the long-abandoned St. John the Baptist church near Journal Square, known as [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/Locations/Undercroft of St. John's - Archado\|Undercroft of St. John's - Archado]]. It is not a home; it is a cell. The space is utterly spartan: a stone slab for rest, a small table with a whetstone for sharpening a collection of antique blades, and a single, heavy book of the Traditions bound in black leather. The air is cold, still, and smells of damp stone and old incense. It is a place of shadow, silence, and grim purpose, perfectly suited to a creature who lives in darkness and serves a merciless law.

### The "[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)\|Brownstone Watch]]" (Ghouls):

In addition to her lieutenants, [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] maintains her ghoul network. These are not thuggish muscle, but rather seemingly normal Hoboken residents. They are her eyes and ears, and they report _everything_ to her. They handle minor threats (like a lone Thin-Blood) themselves, silently and efficiently.

1.  **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)\|Mrs. Agnello]] - The Neighborhood Watch:**
    -   **Look:** A small, elderly Italian-American woman, always dressed in a black dress and comfortable shoes. She is a constant fixture on the Hoboken streets, seen walking "Bambino," her vicious, overfed (and ghouled) bulldog, at all hours of the night, especially between 2 and 4 AM. Her appearance belies a deceptive spryness and strength, a gift of the Vitae.
    -   **Role:** Mrs. Agnello has lived in Hoboken for 70 years and possesses an encyclopedic knowledge of her block—every family, every car, and every secret. She notices *everything*. She is [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]]'s primary agent for observing the brownstone-lined streets, ensuring the Countess's "flock" remains undisturbed. "Bambino" is more than capable of tearing a limb off anyone who gets too close to her or her mistress's interests.
    -   **Quote:** _"Such a shame, that young man. Nice-looking boy. He shouldn't be wandering around so late. It's not safe."_
    -   **Connection:** Ghoul to [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] ([[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/Hoboken Autarkis (JC)\|Hoboken Autarkis]]).

2.  **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/Brownstone Watch (Ghouls)\|Sully]]" - The Gatekeeper:**
    -   **Look:** A quiet, tired-looking man in his late 40s, perpetually seen in a stained blue work shirt and carrying a heavy ring of keys. He seems constantly exhausted and rarely makes eye contact, preferring to blend into the background, an almost invisible fixture of the urban landscape.
    -   **Role:** Sully is the live-in superintendent for a large, pre-war apartment building on Washington Street. He holds master keys to every unit, has unrestricted access to the basement and roof, and sees everyone who comes and goes. He is [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]]'s "utility" man, planting surveillance, securing temporary bolt-holes in empty apartments, and disposing of "trash" (or bodies) in the building's massive, ever-hungry furnace. His intimate knowledge of the building's infrastructure makes him an invaluable asset for discreet operations.
    -   **Quote:** _"...Boiler's actin' up again. Gotta go down. Don't... don't go down there."_
    -   **Connection:** Ghoul to [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] ([[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/Hoboken Autarkis (JC)\|Hoboken Autarkis]]).

### Relationships with Other Factions

While [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] prefers isolation, her lieutenants and ghouls maintain a constant, if unseen, awareness of the Kindred in Jersey City. Their perspectives align with the Countess's motivation for **Sovereignty**, viewing external factions through the lens of Hoboken's stability.

-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]] (Camarilla):** The Countess views Durand as an irritating political headache, a weak ruler whose anemic coterie and the chaos of Jersey City constantly threaten her borders. She tolerates his existence only because New York's Camarilla is unwilling to dislodge her. Her lieutenants share this disdain, seeing him as a relic whose obsession with appearances and physical control makes him vulnerable and a potential threat to Hoboken's pristine order.
-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]] (Camarilla):** A crude enforcer of a flawed system. The Countess's lieutenants acknowledge his street-level effectiveness in keeping Jersey City's chaos contained, preventing it from spilling into Hoboken, but find his methods archaic and his loyalty misplaced.
-   **"Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]] (Camarilla):** A manipulator of secrets and minds. The Countess's lieutenants view him with deep suspicion, wary of any psychological intrusions into Hoboken's order or any information gathering that could threaten its stability or the Countess's privacy.
-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]] (Camarilla):** Irrelevant, a distraction, a source of potential Masquerade breaches that could draw unwanted attention to Hoboken. The Countess's lieutenants have no patience for such frivolity, seeing him as a low-hanging fruit for data extraction.
-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Regent Evelyn Reed\|Regent Evelyn Reed]] (Camarilla):** Her arcane pursuits are a potential source of power or instability that could be exploited, but also a potential threat if her geomancy extends into Hoboken. The Countess's lieutenants would monitor her closely for any encroachment.
-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]]**: The Countess's hatred for the Sabbat's chaos is absolute. Any attempt by them to use Hoboken as a staging ground is met with lethal force by her and her lieutenants.

### The Coterie's Role

You will almost certainly be sent to Hoboken at some point, and it will be a terrifying assignment. [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Russo]] (Durand) might task you with:

-   **Diplomacy:** Begging [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] for neutrality in the war against the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]].
-   **Espionage:** Trying to find a weakness in her defenses (a task Russo's masters in NY are demanding).
-   **"Extradition":** A Camarilla fugitive may flee to Hoboken, forcing you to cross the border and humbly beg [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Hoboken Autarkis/People/The Countess Alexia Vros (Toreador)\|The Countess]] to return them... or risk her wrath by trying to poach them from her domain.
