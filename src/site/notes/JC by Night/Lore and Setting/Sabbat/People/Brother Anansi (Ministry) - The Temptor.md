### **"Brother Anansi" (Ministry** _**antitribu**_**) - The Temptor**

#### **Appearance**

Anansi is a charismatic, smooth-talking man with a wide, disarming, and deeply predatory smile. He dresses like a '90s club promoter or a high-end drug dealer—flashy, confident, and impeccably styled in silk shirts, gold chains, and designer-label jackets. He is the picture of temptation, exuding a magnetism that draws people in, even as it coils around them.

#### **Role and Demeanor**

Anansi is the "Temptor," the "face" and infiltrator for the "Gutter Saints." His job is to find and procure the victims for the pack's rituals. He uses his supernatural powers of corruption and persuasion (Presence and Serpentis) to lure targets from clubs and bars, get them addicted, and "marinate" them in their darkest desires. He isolates them (often at the B-List hotel) before handing them over to Zantosa. He also spreads disinformation on the street, framing the killings as the work of a "Camarilla psycho" to further Kovic's strategic goals.

#### **Motivation: The Ecstasy of Corruption**

Anansi is a true believer in the Ministry's path of liberation through indulgence, but twisted through a Sabbat lens. He is motivated by the act of "freeing" mortals from their morality. He enjoys finding a "good" person and peeling back their layers of self-control, exposing the desperate animal within. Handing them to Zantosa is the final, ecstatic act of "liberation," proving his philosophy that all beings are just one push away from delicious oblivion.

#### **Relationships with the Sabbat**

- **Bishop Kovic:** Anansi is Kovic's most effective infiltrator and propaganda agent, weakening the enemy from within before the "Gutter Saints" even strike.
    
- **Zantosa:** Anansi is the "chef" who selects and prepares the "ingredients" for Zantosa, the "artist." He takes pride in delivering a victim who is "marinated" in sin, making the kill (and the resulting art) all the more potent.
    
- **Bianca "Bones" Rosselini:** Anansi's work makes Bianca's easier. The more corrupted the soul, the more tormented the ghost. He "seasons" the spirits that Bianca will later command.
    

#### **Haven**

Anansi likely doesn't stay in the meatpacking plant, which he would find crude and foul. He maintains his own haven, perhaps a "comped" permanent suite at the B-List hotel itself or a high-end apartment nearby, paid for by his "flock." He meets the pack at their haven to "deliver" his offerings.
