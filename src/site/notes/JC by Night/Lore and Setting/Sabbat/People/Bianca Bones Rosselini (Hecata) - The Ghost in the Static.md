### **Bianca "Bones" Rosselini (Hecata** _**antitribu**_**) - The Ghost in the Static**

#### **Appearance**

Bianca is a pale, morbid woman who embodies '90s grunge fashion. She's often in a torn flannel shirt over a faded band t-shirt (perhaps Nine Inch Nails or Soundgarden), ripped jeans, and scuffed boots. Her constant accessory is a Sony Walkman with bulky headphones, from which she perpetually listens to distorted recordings or pure static—whispers that only she can understand.

#### **Role and Demeanor**

Bianca is the "Ghost" of the "Gutter Saints." As the pack's necromancer, her job is to ensure the "haunting" continues long after Zantosa's physical work is done. She binds the victims' ghosts to the locations of their deaths, creating _actual_ haunted houses. This supernatural terror baffles police and mortals, amplifying the Masquerade breach. She also serves as the pack's intelligence gatherer, interrogating the dead (both fresh victims and old spirits) to learn Camarilla secrets, routes, and haven locations.

#### **Motivation: The Echo of Terror**

Bianca is driven by a morbid fascination with the echoes left by death. She believes the true terror of a moment is not the act itself, but the psychic stain it leaves. She is motivated to "turn up the volume" on these stains, forcing the living to confront the screaming agony of the dead. This alignment with the Sabbat's terror tactics is a perfect expression of her necromantic obsession.

#### **Relationships with the Sabbat**

- **Bishop Kovic:** Kovic is the general who directs her unique talents. She provides him with intelligence gleaned from the other side, making her an invaluable asset he protects.
    
- **Zantosa:** Bianca's work begins where Zantosa's ends. They are two halves of the same terror tactic—Zantosa creates the physical horror, and Bianca creates the spiritual one. It's a pragmatic, assembly-line-of-terror partnership.
    
- **"Brother Anansi":** She likely finds Anansi's methods of corruption interesting, as they "flavor" the souls she later interacts with. She may even provide him with intel from the dead to help him target his next victim.
    

#### **Haven**

Bianca shares the abandoned meatpacking plant with her pack. Her personal space is likely a cold, dark locker room or a small office, filled with her recording equipment, notebooks, and occult paraphernalia, where the static from her Walkman never stops.
