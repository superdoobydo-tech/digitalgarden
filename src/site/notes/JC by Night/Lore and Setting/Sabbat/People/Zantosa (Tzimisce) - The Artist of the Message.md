### **Zantosa (Tzimisce) - The Artist of the Message**

#### **Appearance**

Zantosa is an androgynous, unsettlingly beautiful figure clad in '90s industrial and goth fashion. They favor ripped fishnets, heavy leather, and steel-toed combat boots. Their movements are unnervingly fluid and precise, like a dancer or a surgeon. Most terrifyingly, they maintain a serene, beatific smile, even—and especially—while they "work," viewing their gruesome creations with a sense of profound artistic satisfaction.

#### **Role and Demeanor**

Zantosa is the "Artist" of the "Gutter Saints" pack. As a master flesh-crafter, their role is to create the gruesome, "ritualistic" murder scenes that are the core of the "Front Page Problem." They see their victims as a canvas, not just to be killed, but to be _sculpted_. Each body is a deliberate, horrific message designed to instill maximum terror in the mortals (and Kindred) who find it, thereby shattering the Masquerade and driving the JCPD into a "serial killer" panic.

#### **Motivation: Expression (via Terror)**

Zantosa is driven by the Tzimisce imperative to create, but their art form is terror. They are a true believer in the Sabbat's war, and they see their "sculptures" as their contribution. They are motivated by the desire to craft the perfect "message," a masterpiece of horror so profound it will break the minds of mortals and the will of the Camarilla.

#### **Relationships with the Sabbat**

- **Bishop Kovic:** Zantosa respects Kovic as a patron who appreciates their "art" and provides them with the canvas, the tools, and the divine mission to express themselves.
    
- **"Brother Anansi":** Anansi is their "curator." He finds and procures the "raw materials" (victims) for their work. Zantosa likely views him as a useful, if crude, assistant who preps the canvas.
    
- **Bianca "Bones" Rosselini:** Bianca is the "critic" who "interviews" the artwork after it's finished. Zantosa would have a cold, professional respect for Bianca's ability to add a supernatural echo to their physical creations, completing the "exhibit."
    

#### **Haven**

The "Gutter Saints" share a pack haven: a non-descript, abandoned meatpacking plant near the rail yards. Zantosa likely claimed the main processing floor as their "studio." The location is perfect, chosen for its isolation, drainable floors (for blood), and large industrial freezers (for "materials" and "works in progress").
