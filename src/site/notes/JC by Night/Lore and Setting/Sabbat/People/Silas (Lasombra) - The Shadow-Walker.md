### **Silas (Lasombra) - The Shadow-Walker**

#### **Appearance**

Silas is a young, gaunt man who looks like a '90s skateboarder or punk kid. He is almost always in a black hoodie, a beanie pulled down low, and a pair of scuffed Vans. He seems unassuming, quiet, and sullen—just another face in the urban decay—until he moves with impossible speed or simply vanishes into a patch of shadow.

#### **Role and Demeanor**

Silas is the "Shadow-Walker," one of Bishop Kovic's primary scouts and infiltrators. His role is not frontline combat, but pure espionage. He uses his command of shadows (Obtenebration) to slip past Camarilla defenses, observe targets (like the player's coterie), and find safe invasion routes for Sabbat packs. He is the one you spot watching from a dark alley, only to disappear the moment you turn your head. He is quiet, patient, and lives in the darkness.

#### **Motivation: The Comfort of the Void**

Silas is not a fanatic like Kovic; he is an explorer. He is motivated by the power Obtenebration gives him—the ability to detach, to disappear, to become one with the "living" darkness. The Sabbat's war is simply the perfect environment to practice his "art" and explore the void. He serves Kovic because Kovic encourages him to push his boundaries and slip into places no one else can go.

#### **Relationships with the Sabbat**

- **Bishop Kovic:** Silas is Kovic's "eyes." He has a simple, professional relationship with the Bishop: Kovic gives him a target, and Silas returns with information. Kovic values his total discretion and effectiveness.
    
- **The "Gutter Saints":** Silas is likely aware of the pack and views them as "loud" and "messy." They are the hammer; he is the shadow. He provides the intel that aims them, but he would never want to be part of their gruesome, public "art." He prefers the clean silence of the dark.
    

#### **Haven**

Silas's haven is the projection booth of the abandoned **Loew's [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Locations/Loew's Jersey Theatre - Silas\|Loew Theatre]]** in Journal Square. This massive, crumbling movie palace is a perfect lair for him—it's enormous, opulent in its decay, and, above all, lightless. He has thousands of square feet of shadow to practice his "art," melt into, and use as gateways. He finds the decaying opulence of the theater deeply, ironically beautiful.
