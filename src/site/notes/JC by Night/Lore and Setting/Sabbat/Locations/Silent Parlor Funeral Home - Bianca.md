To call Bianca's haven a "funeral home" is to acknowledge its original purpose, but not its current, chilling function. It is a necromantic workshop, a repository of lingering grief, and a stage for the dead to dance to her morbid tune. Located in a forgotten, blighted residential block, the abandoned Silent Parlor Funeral Home serves as Bianca's personal domain, a place where the veil between life and death is thin, and the whispers of the departed are her constant companions.

**The Approach:**
The journey to The Silent Parlor is a walk through a neighborhood that time has forgotten. Overgrown yards, boarded-up windows, and peeling paint define the street. The funeral home itself is a two-story brick building, its once-dignified facade now marred by decay. The sign, "The Silent Parlor," hangs askew, its letters faded and chipped. The air is unnaturally still, cold, and carries the faint, cloying scent of old embalming fluid, dust, and the ghost of lilies. Entry is subtle, perhaps through a broken basement window or a back service door, avoiding the more obvious, yet heavily secured, main entrance. The silence within is profound, broken only by the distant city hum and the ever-present, low static from Bianca's Walkman.

**The Aesthetic:**
The haven is a place of morbid beauty, where the remnants of human grief are repurposed for supernatural ends.
-   **The Chapel/Viewing Rooms (The Echoing Halls):** The main floor, once dedicated to wakes and services, is now largely stripped of its furnishings. Dust motes dance in the faint light filtering through grimy, boarded-up windows. The lingering aura of countless farewells and profound sorrow permeates the air. Bianca might use these larger spaces for more elaborate bindings, allowing spirits to manifest or to "listen" to the echoes of past mourners, tuning her necromantic senses to the residual psychic energy.
-   **The Embalming Room/Morgue (The Sanctum of Whispers):** This is Bianca's true inner sanctum, located in the basement. It's a cold, tiled room with drains in the floor and a heavy, stainless-steel table at its center. The air here is even colder, carrying a stronger, metallic tang. This is where she conducts her most potent rituals, where she can be closest to the recently deceased or their lingering spirits. The constant static from her Walkman is not just background noise; it's a deliberate filter, a way to drown out the mundane world and tune into the spectral frequencies, or perhaps to record the actual whispers of the dead.

**The Sanctum & Furnishings:**
The embalming room is equipped for necromantic craft, not comfort.
-   The stainless-steel table serves as her primary workspace, often adorned with chalk circles, candles, grave dirt, and a collection of bones or personal effects salvaged from the deceased.
-   Her Walkman and headphones are essential, often connected to crude recording devices or modified radios, attempting to capture or amplify spectral voices.
-   A makeshift cot or sleeping bag might be tucked into a corner, offering minimal rest.
-   The walls, tiled and grimy, might be covered in cryptic notes, names, dates, or symbols related to her necromantic studies and the spirits she has bound or interrogated.
-   Various jars and containers hold components for her rituals: dried herbs, preserved organs, or samples of blood and soil.

**The Focus:**
The Silent Parlor is Bianca's laboratory of the afterlife. Her focus is entirely on her necromantic craft: binding spirits, interrogating the dead for Camarilla secrets, and creating the "actual haunted houses" that further terrify mortals and baffle police. She views the lingering energies of death and grief as raw materials, shaping them to serve the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]]'s agenda of terror and destabilization. Her power comes from her intimate connection to the dead and her ability to manipulate their spectral forms.

**Function and Security:**
Security relies on the haven's inherent creepiness, Bianca's powers, and the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]]'s tactical awareness.
-   **Natural Deterrence:** The abandoned, dilapidated funeral home naturally deters most mortals, who find its atmosphere unsettling.
-   **Spectral Guardians:** Bianca's necromantic powers allow her to bind spirits to the location, creating illusions of haunting or even spectral guardians to scare off or attack intruders.
-   **Isolation & Decay:** The building's location in a blighted area and its state of decay provide natural obscurity and make it an unlikely target for thorough investigation.
-   **Pack Support:** While her personal haven, it's still within the broader [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Sabbat/Sabbat\|Sabbat]] territory, and the Gutter Saints would be aware of its importance and likely have protocols for its defense.
-   **Strategic Exits:** Multiple hidden exits, perhaps through the basement to old utility lines, forgotten crypts, or a back alley, ensure Bianca can vanish if her haven is compromised, leaving only the lingering echoes of the dead behind.
