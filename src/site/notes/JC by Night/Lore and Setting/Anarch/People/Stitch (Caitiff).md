### **Stitch (Caitiff) - The Anarch Fixer**

#### **Appearance**
Stitch presents a formidable and pragmatic image, heavily influenced by a '90s "riot grrl" aesthetic. Her head is shaved, a practical choice that adds to her no-nonsense demeanor. She is almost never seen without her signature leather jacket and combat boots, attire that is as much a uniform as it is a personal statement. A well-worn, '90s-era first-aid kit is a constant accessory, hinting at her crucial role within the Anarch movement and the rough nature of their unlife. Her eyes are sharp, constantly assessing, and rarely betray emotion.

#### **Role and Demeanor**
**The Fixer & Enforcer.** Stitch is the indispensable medic, quartermaster, and enforcer of the Jersey City [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Anarchs (JC)\|Anarchs (JC)]]. Lacking a clan, her loyalty is entirely to the "brotherhood" itself, which she views as the only family she has. This fierce dedication drives her to be the one who literally holds the group together. She is the muscle that backs up [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Red Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron\|Red Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron]]'s speeches, keeping the Anarchs in line and "disciplining" any Thin-Bloods or Drudges who step out of line. Using a combination of scavenged medical supplies and hard-won street knowledge, she patches up any Anarch who gets into a scrape, no questions asked. Beyond her medical skills, she "fixes" problems: procuring clean blood sources, untraceable transportation, or specific equipment through a network of contacts and sheer resourcefulness. She is the quiet, pragmatic core of the group, ensuring that for all their revolutionary fervor, they have the practical means to survive another night. She is less a warrior and more a battlefield surgeon and quartermaster, working tirelessly behind the lines to keep the revolution's soldiers in the fight.

#### **Motivation: Survival of the Brotherhood**
Stitch is driven by a deep-seated, almost maternal need to protect the Anarchs. Having found a place and purpose within Red's movement, she is fiercely dedicated to its survival. She believes in the Anarch cause not out of abstract ideology, but because it offers a tangible community and a chance for the marginalized (like herself and the Thin-Bloods) to thrive. Her actions are always geared towards strengthening the Anarchs' practical capabilities and ensuring their continued existence against the oppressive Camarilla.

Scrap relies on Stitch's resourcefulness for supplies and intel, and they likely coordinate closely on managing the Anarchs' assets and personnel.

#### **Haven: The Suture Room**
Stitch has established her haven and clinic, known as [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Locations/Static Age - Suture Room (Stitch)\|The Suture Room]], in the back room of a defunct, free-for-all punk club near the Holland Tunnel. This location is strategically chosen for its accessibility and the constant, deafening thrum of music from the still-active club, which provides the perfect cover, masking any sounds of her "clinical" work. Her haven is less a home and more a functional, grim workshop dedicated to the bloody, necessary business of Anarch survival, equipped with scavenged medical supplies and a hidden escape route into the sewer system.
