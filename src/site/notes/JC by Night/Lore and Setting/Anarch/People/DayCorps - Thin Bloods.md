### **The Daylight Corps - The Anarchs' Unseen Advantage**

#### **Overview**
The Daylight Corps is not a single individual, but the collective term for the Thin-Bloods and trusted mortal agents who form the backbone of the Jersey City [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Anarchs (JC)\|Anarchs (JC)]]'s daytime operations. This isn't a dark secret; it's the Anarchs' open, radical strategy and their single greatest advantage against the slumbering Camarilla. While the Camarilla hunts Thin-Bloods and the Sabbat uses them as cannon fodder, [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Red Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron\|Red Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron]] offers them sanctuary, respect, and a vital role in his revolution.

#### **Role and Demeanor**
The Daylight Corps are the Anarchs' high-risk, high-reward branch. They are the eyes, ears, and hands of the movement during the sunlit hours when most Kindred are forced into torpor. They run messages, perform daytime surveillance, manage cash drops, and handle all the logistics that the Camarilla cannot. This makes the Anarchs incredibly efficient and responsive, giving them a crucial 24-hour operational advantage in their "War of Attrition." Their demeanor is often a mix of high-strung vigilance, paranoia, and fierce loyalty to the cause that offers them a place.

#### **Motivation: Sanctuary, Purpose, and Survival**
The Thin-Bloods of the Daylight Corps are driven by the unique deal [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Red Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron\|Red Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron]] offers: protection, community, and a vital purpose in a world that otherwise rejects or exploits them. They willingly undertake dangerous daytime tasks because the Anarch "brotherhood" provides the only true sanctuary and respect they've ever known. Their motivation is deeply personal – to survive, to belong, and to contribute to a revolution that promises a better future for all marginalized Kindred.

#### **Relationships within the Anarchs**
The Daylight Corps operates under the direct command of [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Red Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron\|Red Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron]] and his lieutenants, forming a crucial, if often precarious, part of the Anarch hierarchy.
*   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Red Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron\|Red Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron]]**: Red is their protector and the architect of their purpose. He offers them the deal no one else will, and they respond with fierce, if sometimes fearful, loyalty. He views them as an invaluable "resource" in his war against the Prince.
*   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Scrap (Gangrel)\|Scrap (Gangrel)]]**: Scrap rules over the Daylight Corps with an "iron grip." Her pacts with them are "chains forged from a predator's instinct for weakness," ensuring "absolute obedience." She views them as "invaluable, high-risk assets" and is not above making an "object lesson" out of those who fail, ensuring discipline and efficiency.
*   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Jinx (Malkavian)\|Jinx (Malkavian)]]**: Jinx manages many of the "Drudges" (including Thin-Bloods) through a "chaotic mix of genuine fear and a barrage of confusing, contradictory orders." He oversees their activities related to protection rackets and money laundering, leveraging their daytime mobility for financial gain.
*   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Stitch (Caitiff)\|Stitch (Caitiff)]]**: Stitch is responsible for "disciplining" Thin-Bloods who step out of line, ensuring their reliability for daytime operations. She also acts as their medic, patching them up after scrapes, which further solidifies their dependence and loyalty to the "brotherhood."
*   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/DJ Trip (Toreador)\|DJ Trip (Toreador)]]**: Trip's broadcasts are often aimed at galvanizing disenfranchised Thin-Bloods, offering them a "cause" and a "community." He is the voice that draws new recruits into the Daylight Corps.

#### **Key NPCs**
*   **"Flicker" (Thin-Blood):** The high-strung "dispatcher" for the Corps. From a blacked-out room, Flicker spends their days on a '90s-era computer, coordinating the other Thin-Bloods via pagers and coded messages on internet bulletin boards.
*   **"Wheels" (Thin-Blood):** The priority messenger. "Wheels" is a '90s bike messenger who, in exchange for protection, now runs the Anarchs' most sensitive daytime deliveries. He's high on the "cause" and his own adrenaline, knowing he's a vital part of the movement.
*   **"Smudge" (Thin-Blood):** The logistics manager. "Smudge" maintains the Anarchs' network of "daytime safe-houses" (storage units, condemned basements, etc.) and moves assets between them. They are quiet, paranoid, and invaluable.
