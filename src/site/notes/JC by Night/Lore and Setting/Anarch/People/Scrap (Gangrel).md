### **Scrap (Gangrel) - The Iron Will of the Anarchs**

#### **Appearance**
Scrap is the embodiment of feral energy, contained within a wiry and tough frame. Her entire presence evokes the image of a '90s alley cat—watchful, agile, and ready to pounce. She dresses for function over form, favoring practical, layered, and often torn clothing that allows for unrestricted movement. Her hands are never still, and her sharp eyes seem to track multiple things at once, a clear sign of a predator's constant, instinctual awareness of her surroundings.

#### **Role and Demeanor**
**Second-in-Command & Pact Master.** Scrap is [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Red Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron\|Red Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron]]'s undisputed right-hand, the cold, pragmatic will that ensures the Anarch revolution keeps beating. She is the primary enforcer of Anarch territorial borders, patrolling the Docks and "negotiating" with any Kindred who wanders into their space unannounced. Her most crucial role, however, is ruling over the "Daylight Corps"—the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Anarchs (JC)\|Anarchs (JC)]]'s essential network of Thin-Bloods and mortal agents. Her pacts are not agreements; they are chains forged from a predator's instinct for weakness, leveraging it to ensure absolute obedience. Loyalty is a currency paid in service, and the price for defaulting is steep and final. She is not above making an object lesson out of a failed agent, ensuring that the stories of what happens to those who cross her circulate freely among the dockworkers and street informants she controls. She is less a warrior and more a battlefield commander, culling the herd to ensure only the strongest and most loyal assets remain to protect the Anarchs from the dangers of the day.

#### **Motivation: Pragmatic Survival & Territorial Expansion**
Scrap is driven by a brutal, animalistic pragmatism. Her loyalty to Red and the Anarch cause stems from a deep-seated belief that it offers the most viable path to survival and expansion for her kind in Jersey City. She is focused on the tangible: securing territory, managing resources, and eliminating threats. She sees the "Daylight Corps" as the Anarchs' single greatest advantage in their "War of Attrition" against the Camarilla, and she protects this asset with ruthless efficiency.

#### **Haven: The Warren**
Scrap's haven, known as [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Locations/The Warren (Scrap)\|The Warren (Scrap)]], is a perfect reflection of her nature: a fortified nest built within a cluster of old, rusted-out shipping containers at the Port Jersey docks. She has transformed this industrial graveyard into a defensible, maze-like warren. It is a practical and deadly labyrinth, serving not only as the hub for her mortal network but also as the final destination for those who have outlived their usefulness. From within this sensory web, she is aware of every tremor in her steel kingdom, a beast in the heart of the labyrinth, constantly patrolling and defending her territory.
