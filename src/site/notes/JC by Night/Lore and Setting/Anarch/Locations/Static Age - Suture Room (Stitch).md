The Suture Room: Haven Details

To call [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Stitch (Caitiff)\|Stitch (Caitiff)]]'s haven a "clinic" is to grant it a dignity it doesn't possess. It is a workshop, a trauma bay, and a bolt-hole, hidden in the guts of a perpetually raging punk club near the Holland Tunnel. It is a monument to brutal, practical survival.

**The Approach:**
The journey is an assault on the senses. One must first push through the main floor of "Static Age" a chaotic press of sweaty bodies, sticky floors, and the bone-jarring thrum of live music. There is no private entrance. The filter is the chaos itself. Tucked away in a dark corner, past overflowing bathrooms, is a heavy, graffiti-scarred steel door marked "BOILER ROOM." A specific, coded knock is the only key.

**The Aesthetic:**
The first impression upon entering is the sudden, muffled quiet, where the club's roar becomes a dull, rhythmic heartbeat. The air is thick with the acrid cocktail of antiseptic, stale blood, and damp concrete. The space is cramped and cold. There are no windows. The only light comes from a single, buzzing fluorescent strip overhead that flickers erratically, casting long, dancing shadows and rendering everything in a harsh, clinical glare. This is a place stripped of all comfort, designed for one purpose: work.

**The Workshop & Furnishings:**
The space is a hoard of life-saving junk. The centerpiece is not a piece of art, but a heavy, stainless-steel operating table—likely stolen from a morgue—bolted to the center of the floor. A crude drain has been hammered into the concrete beneath it. The walls are lined with bowing metal shelves crammed with:
-   Expired blood bags and IV drips, sourced from raided clinics or desperate contacts.
-   Trays of mismatched surgical tools, sterilized in a pressure cooker on a hot plate.
-   A bank of car batteries, wired together to power her most essential equipment, ensuring she's never reliant on the club's stolen electricity.
-   Stacks of burner phones, clean clothes in vacuum-sealed bags, and fake IDs.

Every object is functional, scavenged, and maintained with a pragmatist's care.

**The Focus:**
Unlike the Prince's outward-facing view of his domain, [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Stitch (Caitiff)\|Stitch]]'s haven is entirely internal. There are no windows to the outside world. The only view is the one directly under the harsh fluorescent light: the patient on the table. The world is reduced to the immediate, bloody task at hand—a bullet to be dug out, a wound to be cauterized, a broken body to be put back together. Her domain is not the city, but the flesh and bone of her coterie.

**Function and Security:**
Security here is brutal and low-tech, a reflection of Anarch philosophy.
-   The primary defense is the club itself: a chaotic, anonymous buffer of mortals.
-   The steel door is reinforced with a length of rebar that serves as a deadbolt.
-   A hidden emergency exit, a crumbling section of the foundation, leads directly into a forgotten sewer access tunnel—a guaranteed escape route.

This is not a fortress of elegant control, but a bunker built from necessity. It is ugly, efficient, and utterly dedicated to keeping the revolution's soldiers in the fight.
