# Faction Briefing: The Jersey City Anarchs

**Vibe:** "No Gods, No Masters, No Princes. Just Us."

### Overview: The Pragmatic Revolution

The Anarchs are the "third way" in Jersey City, a local, grassroots alternative to the Camarilla's ancient, aristocratic tyranny and the Sabbat's fanatical, inhuman crusade. Led by the charismatic "Red" Valenti, they preach a message of brotherhood, equality, and a city for the common Kindred. Their explosive growth is due to one simple, radical strategy: **they "protect" the Thin-Bloods.** While the Camarilla hunts them and the Sabbat uses them as cannon fodder, "Red" offers them sanctuary, respect, and a vital role in the revolution. In return, the Thin-Bloods *willingly* handle the Anarchs' daytime operations, giving them an unmatched, 24-hour advantage. "Red" has moved from protest to conquest, transforming his "union" into a well-oiled insurgency that actively seizes and holds territory, establishing fortified Anarch "Free Zones."

### Core Territory: The Working-Class Heart

The Anarchs control the parts of the city the Camarilla has to *work* in but doesn't want to *live* in.
-   **The Docks / Port Jersey:** This is the heart of "Red's" power. His mortal-world union contacts give him influence over the longshoremen. The Anarchs control smuggling routes and use the endless maze of shipping containers for temporary havens and storage. This area is heavily patrolled by [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Scrap (Gangrel)\|Scrap (Gangrel)]].
-   **Liberty State Park:** Their "public square" and neutral ground. "Red" often holds soapbox speeches here at night, near the abandoned CRRNJ Terminal. The symbolism of the Statue of Liberty is not lost on him.
-   **The Heights:** This is their main "community" and a *hotly* contested territory. While the Camarilla claims it, the Anarchs are the ones with roots in the working-class neighborhoods, running local rackets and maintaining communal havens in the triple-decker apartment buildings. [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/DJ Trip (Toreador)\|DJ Trip (Toreador)]]'s broadcast booth and [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Stitch (Caitiff)\|Stitch (Caitiff)]]'s suture room are located here.

### Key Figures

1.  **"Red" Valenti (Brujah) - The Insurgent Baron:**
    -   **Look:** A passionate, forceful man who looks like he just got off a 12-hour shift. In the 90s, he's often in a worn flannel shirt over a faded union t-shirt, jeans, and work boots. He has a powerful voice and a handshake that could crush rocks.
    -   **Role:** The undisputed Baron of a significant portion of Jersey City. He is the frontline of a territorial war, proving that the Anarchs can govern more effectively than the Ivory Tower.
    -   **Motivation:** **The War of Attrition & A Better World.** Valenti is actively dismantling [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]]'s domain from the ground up, seeing his detached rule as a fatal weakness. His goal is to bleed the Camarilla dry until the Prince is left ruling a penthouse and nothing else.
    -   **Tactics of Encroachment:** He uses infiltration, economic strangulation (labor strikes, zoning problems, intimidation), and surgical violence (destroying havens, ambushing Kindred) to seize territory, then establishes his own people and offers a better deal to locals.
    -   **Haven:** His office above [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Locations/The Iron Rail (Red)\|The Iron Rail (Red)]] is a full-fledged command center, a union hall and war room for his growing empire.

2.  **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Scrap (Gangrel)\|Scrap (Gangrel)]]" (Gangrel) - The Iron Will of the Anarchs:**
    -   **Look:** Wiry and tough, with a feral energy, like a '90s alley cat. Dresses in practical, layered, and often torn clothing. Her hands are never still, and her sharp eyes track everything at once.
    -   **Role:** Second-in-Command & Pact Master. Red's undisputed right-hand, the cold, pragmatic will that ensures the revolution keeps beating. She enforces Anarch territorial borders and rules over the Daylight Corps with an iron grip, ensuring absolute obedience through pacts forged from a predator's instinct for weakness.
    -   **Motivation:** Pragmatic Survival & Territorial Expansion. Driven by a brutal, animalistic pragmatism, focused on securing territory, managing resources, and eliminating threats to the Anarchs' survival and expansion.
    -   **Haven:** [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Locations/The Warren (Scrap)\|The Warren (Scrap)]], a fortified nest within a maze of rusted-out shipping containers at the Port Jersey docks, serving as a hub for her mortal network and a final destination for failed agents.

3.  **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/DJ Trip (Toreador)\|DJ Trip (Toreador)]]" (Toreador *antitribu*) - The Voice of the Insurgency:**
    -   **Look:** A whirlwind of '90s counter-culture: baggy jeans, a faded "Rage Against the Machine" t-shirt, a beanie, and bulky headphones. A deliberate rejection of Camarilla elegance.
    -   **Role:** The self-proclaimed "Voice of the Revolution," chief propagandist and recruiter. He operates a pirate radio station, spinning grunge and hip-hop with fiery anti-Camarilla rants and coded messages to galvanize disenfranchised Neonates and Thin-Bloods.
    -   **Motivation:** Inciting Rebellion & Building the Brotherhood. Driven by a fervent belief in the Anarch cause and a deep-seated desire to dismantle the Prince's oppressive rule, using his broadcasts as a weapon.
    -   **Haven:** "The Booth" within [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Locations/The Booth (Trip)\|The Booth (Trip)]], a sound-proofed basement room in a condemned building in The Heights, powered by stolen electricity.

4.  **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Stitch (Caitiff)\|Stitch (Caitiff)]]" (Caitiff) - The Anarch Fixer:**
    -   **Look:** Pragmatic, no-nonsense, with a shaved head and a '90s "riot grrl" aesthetic. Always in a leather jacket, combat boots, and carrying a well-worn first-aid kit.
    -   **Role:** The indispensable medic, quartermaster, and enforcer. Lacking a clan, her loyalty is entirely to the "brotherhood." She patches up Anarchs, procures clean blood sources, untraceable transportation, and equipment, and "disciplines" Thin-Bloods who step out of line.
    -   **Motivation:** Survival of the Brotherhood. Driven by a deep-seated, almost maternal need to protect the Anarchs, believing their cause offers a tangible community and a chance for the marginalized to thrive.
    -   **Haven:** [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Locations/Static Age - Suture Room (Stitch)\|The Suture Room]], a grim workshop in the back room of a defunct punk club near the Holland Tunnel, chosen for its accessibility and the music's cover.

5.  **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/People/Jinx (Malkavian)\|Jinx (Malkavian)]]" (Malkavian) - The Chaotic Calculator:**
    -   **Look:** A twitchy, fast-talking numbers runner in a Members Only jacket, gold chain, and a constantly beeping pager. His eyes often unfocus as if listening to unseen conversations.
    -   **Role:** The "Bagman," the chaotic but effective financial engine. He masterminds protection rackets, launders cash, and manages "Drudges" (ghouls and mortals) through fear and confusing orders.
    -   **Motivation:** Order in Chaos & Anarch Prosperity. Driven by a Malkavian need to find patterns in the underworld's financial flows, ensuring the Anarchs' financial health through his unpredictable but accurate schemes.
    -   **Haven:** The back office of a 24/7 check-cashing establishment in Journal Square, acquired by subtly driving the mortal owners insane.

### The Daylight Corps

This isn't a dark secret; it's the Anarchs' open strategy and their single greatest advantage.
-   **The Deal:** The Anarchs offer protection, community, and shared feeding grounds to any Thin-Blood who joins the "brotherhood." In exchange, the Thin-Bloods use their unique (if limited) ability to operate during the day to run the Anarchs' mortal-world affairs.
-   **Their "Role":** They are the "Daylight Corps," a high-risk, high-reward branch of the movement. They run messages, perform daytime surveillance, manage cash drops, and handle all the logistics that the Camarilla (who are all fast asleep) can't. This makes the Anarchs incredibly efficient and responsive. Their demeanor is often a mix of high-strung vigilance, paranoia, and fierce loyalty to the cause that offers them a place.
-   **Key NPCs:**
    -   **"Flicker" (Thin-Blood):** The high-strung "dispatcher" for the Corps, coordinating Thin-Bloods via pagers and coded messages on internet bulletin boards from a blacked-out room.
    -   **"Wheels" (Thin-Blood):** The priority messenger, a '90s bike messenger running sensitive daytime deliveries, high on the "cause" and adrenaline.
    -   **"Smudge" (Thin-Blood):** The logistics manager, maintaining the network of "daytime safe-houses" (storage units, condemned basements, etc.) and moving assets between them. Quiet, paranoid, and invaluable.

### The Coterie's Role

The Anarchs are a constant source of trouble and opportunity.
-   **Rivals:** You will clash with them over territory and influence in The Heights.
-   **Temptation:** "Red" may offer you *real* respect, something Durand (and Manhattan) will never give you.
-   **Strategic Advantage:** You will quickly learn the Anarchs can get things done *during the day*. Need a message passed at noon? Need eyes on a building when the sun is up? They can do it. This makes them incredibly tempting as allies... and incredibly dangerous as foes.
-   **Strategic Weakness:** The Daylight Corps is the key to their power, but it's also their greatest vulnerability. If the Camarilla (or the Sabbat) can identify and *eliminate* these Thin-Bloods, the Anarchs' entire operation would grind to a halt.
-   **Intel Broker (Plot Hook):** "Red" is the one who gives you the "anonymous tip" in "The Front Page Problem." He wants the Sabbat gone, and he's perfectly happy to let the Camarilla's Ancilla do the dirty work.
