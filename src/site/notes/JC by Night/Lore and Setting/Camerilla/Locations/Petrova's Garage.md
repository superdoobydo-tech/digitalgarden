#### Petrova's Garage: Haven Details

If [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]]'s haven is a sterile throne in the sky, Sheriff [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Petrova]]'s is the city's bloody, beating heart, buried in the industrial grime near the Holland Tunnel. It is not a place of judgment; it is a place of consequence.

**The Exterior & Approach:**
The building is a squat, anonymous cinderblock structure with a corrugated steel roof, indistinguishable from the dozen other warehouses and body shops in the district. The sign, "PETROVA'S AUTO & REPAIR," is faded and flickers erratically. The constant rumble of trucks and the smell of diesel fumes provide a permanent cloak of noise and scent. The only indication of its true nature is the sheer lack of windows and the heavy-duty, reinforced roll-up garage door that looks capable of stopping a truck.

**The Main Floor (The Shop):**
The interior smells of motor oil, hot metal, and stale coffee. The air is thick and cool. The vast, open space is a working garage, with cars in various states of disassembly sitting under harsh fluorescent lights that buzz and hum. The concrete floor is a map of dark stains, and the walls are lined with meticulously organized, but heavily used, tools. This is a place of function over form. Every wrench, every hydraulic lift, every welding torch is a tool, and potentially, a weapon.

**The Armory (The "Tool Shed"):**
Behind a door marked "PRIVATE" is a converted walk-in freezer. The temperature is kept just above freezing, and the walls are lined with weapon racks. This is not an arsenal of elegant blades or high-tech firearms. It is a collection of brutal, practical instruments:
-   Pump-action shotguns and heavy-caliber handguns, all meticulously cleaned.
-   A variety of melee weapons: steel baseball bats, crowbars, and several tire irons of different weights.
-   Custom-made weapons forged in the shop itself: sharpened leaf springs used as short swords, heavy chains, and clubs made from reinforced rebar.

**The Office:**
A small, cage-like office sits on a raised platform in the corner, overlooking the entire shop floor. It's enclosed in thick chain-link fencing. Inside is a battered metal desk, a single chair, a perpetually brewing pot of coffee, and a bank of old, flickering CRT monitors displaying security feeds from the perimeter. This is Petrova's perch, where she can watch, wait, and work.

**The Holding Cell (The "Pit"):**
The haven's most infamous feature is hidden beneath a standard-looking hydraulic car lift in the center of the garage. When activated, the lift raises a section of the concrete floor, revealing a steep metal staircase leading down into a sub-basement. The "Pit" is a soundproofed, lightless concrete bunker. There is a single heavy steel door with no internal handle and three separate, heavy-duty deadbolts. A small, grated drain in the center of the floor is the only feature. It is a place designed to break a Kindred's spirit through absolute sensory deprivation and the crushing certainty of their imprisonment.
