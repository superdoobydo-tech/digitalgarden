#### The Montgomery Wellness Center: Haven Details

"Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Thorne]]'s haven is a masterclass in misdirection. It is not a fortress of steel and concrete, but a fortress of perception and secrets, hiding in plain sight in a respectable neighborhood.

**The Exterior & Approach:**
The clinic is a beautifully maintained three-story brownstone on a quiet, tree-lined street bordering Montgomery Park. A small, tasteful brass plaque next to the heavy oak door reads: "The Montgomery Wellness Center - By Appointment Only." There are no large signs, no advertisements. The windows are made of frosted, one-way glass, allowing light in but preventing anyone from seeing inside. To any mortal observer, it is the epitome of a discreet, high-end private practice.

**The First Floor (The Mask of Sanity):**
The public-facing area is designed to soothe and disarm. The waiting room is decorated in calming neutral tones, with comfortable, overstuffed armchairs, soft ambient lighting, and a faint, pleasant scent of lavender and old books. A subtle white noise generator hums almost imperceptibly, masking all conversation. There is no receptionist; a simple, elegant sign instructs visitors to take a seat, and [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]] will be with them shortly. This entire floor is a carefully constructed illusion of normalcy, a buffer between the mortal world and the haven's true purpose.

**The Second Floor (The Office & The Web):**
[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Thorne]]'s primary consultation room is on the second floor. It is meticulously soundproofed. The room is comfortable but psychologically potent. Two identical, high-backed leather chairs face each other with no desk in between, creating a sense of forced intimacy and equality. The walls are lined with bookshelves, but they are filled with untitled, leather-bound volumes—anonymized case files and journals. The only art is a series of complex, abstract Rorschach inkblots framed on the wall. This is the room where secrets are offered, anxieties are dissected, and information is traded. It is a confessional and an interrogation chamber disguised as a therapist's office.

**The Basement (The Sanctum):**
Accessed via a hidden door disguised as a bookshelf in his office, a narrow staircase leads down to the haven's core. The basement is not a dungeon; it is a vault. The entire space is a Faraday cage, blocking all electronic signals. The walls, floor, and ceiling are lined with acoustic foam panels over layers of sound-dampening material, creating an environment of absolute, unnerving silence. The room is spartan, containing only two simple chairs and a single, low-wattage overhead light. This is where [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Thorne]] holds his most sensitive sessions, where a Kindred can scream, rage, or confess their deepest fears with the absolute certainty that no one outside these four walls will ever hear.

**Security and Neutral Ground:**
The clinic's security is not based on physical defenses, but on the principle of mutually assured destruction. [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]] holds the secrets of every faction in the city. An attack on his haven would be an attack on the city's entire network of information. The secrets he holds are the only weapons he needs, and this unspoken threat is what truly makes The Montgomery Wellness Center the one place in Jersey City where anyone can speak freely—as long as they are willing to pay the price.
