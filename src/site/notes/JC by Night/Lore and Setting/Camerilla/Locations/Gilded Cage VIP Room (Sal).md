Here's the updated description for [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Gilded Cage VIP Room (Sal)\|Gilded Cage VIP Room (Sal)]], taking into account Sal's new character as the "Desperate Promoter" leveraging his club's Elysium status:

#### The Gilded Cage VIP Room: Haven Details

[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]]'s haven is a perfect reflection of the man himself: a cheap, desperate imitation of luxury and security. It is not a fortress or a sanctum; it is a gilded cage where he hides, watching his failing domain through a one-way mirror, desperately trying to make his Prince-sanctioned Elysium a success.

**The Approach:**
Access is gained by navigating the sticky floors of _The Gilded Cage_, pushing through the sparse, listless crowd, and approaching a velvet rope that's seen better days. A single, bored-looking bouncer guards a short, dingy hallway that leads to a door with a tarnished brass plaque reading "MANAGEMENT." The muffled, incessant *thump-thump-thump* of the club's bass is a constant, oppressive presence, a reminder of the fading energy Sal is so desperate to rekindle.

**The Office Interior:**
The air inside is a suffocating cocktail of stale cigarette smoke, spilled liquor, and Sal's overpowering cologne. The room is small and cramped, an attempt at a high-roller's lounge that falls miserably short.
-   **Decor:** The walls are covered in faux-wood paneling, and a cheap, worn-out Persian rug covers most of the floor. A cracked, black pleather sofa is pushed against one wall.
-   **The Desk:** A large, ostentatious desk, covered in a veneer that is peeling at the corners, dominates the space. It's cluttered with stacks of unpaid invoices, liquor receipts, and half-finished promotional flyers for drink specials that will never bring in the crowds he needs. Amidst the chaos are hastily scribbled notes on potential Kindred guests, ideas for "exclusive" Elysium events, and desperate calculations on how to make the club profitable again.
-   **The Vibe:** The only light comes from a single green banker's lamp on the desk, casting long, paranoid shadows across the room. It feels less like an office and more like a bookie's back room, where Sal plots his next desperate move to save his club and his standing.

**The One-Way Mirror:**
The haven's main feature is a large, one-way mirror that takes up most of the wall opposite the desk. This is Sal's window to his kingdom. He spends hours here, perched like a nervous gargoyle, watching the dancers, the drinkers, and especially the occasional Kindred visitor. He's not admiring the view; he's desperately scanning for information, gossip, trouble—anything he can use to curry favor with [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]], identify potential high-value patrons for his Elysium, or get a leg up on his rivals to ensure his club's survival.

**Security & The Escape Route:**
Sal's security is born of pure paranoia and is predictably flawed.
-   **The Door:** The reinforced steel door has three separate deadbolts, but the doorframe itself is flimsy wood. It's more for show than actual protection, designed to impress and intimidate rather than truly secure.
-   **The Escape Hatch:** The "less-than-secret" escape hatch is a large ventilation grate hidden under the cheap Persian rug. The rug is perpetually rumpled in one corner, a dead giveaway to anyone looking for it. The grate leads into the building's greasy, cramped ventilation system—a claustrophobic and undignified escape route for a desperate man.
