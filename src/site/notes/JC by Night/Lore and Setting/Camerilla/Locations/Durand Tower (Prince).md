### Durand Tower: Haven Details

To call [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Durand]]'s penthouse a "home" is a categorical error. It is a statement, a gallery, and a throne room, located on the highest floor of one of Paulus Hook's most exclusive glass towers.

**The Approach:**
The journey itself is a filter. A private elevator, requiring a keycard and a biometric scan, ascends in unnerving silence. The doors open not into a foyer, but directly into the main space, creating a sudden, disorienting exposure to the vastness within and without.

**The Aesthetic:**
The first impression is one of absolute, sterile white. The floors are polished white marble that seamlessly meets stark white walls, all illuminated by cold, recessed gallery lighting. There is no clutter, no personal memorabilia, no sign that anyone truly *lives* here. The air is cool, dry, and filtered, carrying the faint, clean scent of ozone. The silence is profound; the city's roar is completely sealed out, leaving a vacuum that makes a visitor acutely aware of their own breathing and heartbeat.

**The Gallery & Furnishings:**
The space is furnished not for comfort, but for display. A single, severe black leather chaise lounge sits angled towards the windows, more a piece of sculpture than a place to rest. The art is brutally minimalist or starkly conceptual—a single, massive canvas painted in a gradient of grey, a sculpture of twisted black iron that seems to absorb the light, a series of photographs depicting architectural decay in excruciatingly sharp detail. Each piece is isolated, given an entire wall to itself, demanding contemplation but offering no emotional warmth.

**The View:**
The floor-to-ceiling windows are the haven's true centerpiece. They are not just windows; they are a statement. Made of reinforced, optically perfect bulletproof glass, they frame the glittering skyline of Manhattan and the dark waters of the Hudson as a living masterpiece—one that [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Durand]] possesses but does not touch. From this height, the streets of Jersey City are just abstract patterns of light, and the people—mortal and Kindred alike—are insignificant insects. This is how the Prince sees his domain: from a cold, beautiful, and untouchable distance.

**Function and Security:**
This is not a fortress built from fear, but a monument to control. Security is an aesthetic, not a panic measure. It is silent and integrated:
-   Weight sensors in the polished floors.
-   Biometric locks disguised as minimalist door handles.
-   A climate control system that can seal the entire residence in seconds.

The haven is designed to be unbreachable through elegance, not brute force. Any private chambers are hidden behind seamless wall panels, and one can assume they are just as spartan and devoid of personal comfort as the main gallery.
