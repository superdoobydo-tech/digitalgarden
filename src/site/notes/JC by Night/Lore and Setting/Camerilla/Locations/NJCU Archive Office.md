#### The Archive Office: Haven Details

[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Regent Evelyn Reed\|Regent Evelyn Reed]]'s haven is a fortress of knowledge, secured not by brute force, but by obscurity and institutional bureaucracy. It is a place of quiet, intense work, where the secrets of the city are not traded, but meticulously researched and cataloged.

**The Approach:**
The journey to her haven is a descent through layers of academic mundanity. One must first enter the bustling, brightly-lit main university library, a place of mortal activity and public access. From there, a staff keycard is required to access a series of sterile, quiet corridors leading to a service elevator. The elevator descends past the main stacks, past the ground floor, into the deep sub-basement. The air grows cool and still, and the smell of old paper and climate-control systems becomes dominant. The final corridor is a long, narrow passage lined with rolling archival shelves. Her office is at the very end, marked by a simple, unassuming door with a small plastic sign: "E. Reed - Visiting Researcher."

**The Office Interior:**
The room is small, cramped, and utilitarian, every inch dedicated to her work. The only light comes from a harsh, adjustable desk lamp that illuminates a sea of paper.
-   **The Scent:** The air is a dry, scholarly mix of aging paper, bookbinding glue, and the faint, sharp tang of chemical reagents.
-   **The Workspace:** There is no couch or comfortable chair. A large, scarred wooden table serves as her primary desk, currently covered with an open 19th-century map of Jersey City, held down by various geological samples. A powerful, modern computer terminal sits next to it, its screen displaying arcane text, a stark contrast to the ancient documents surrounding it.
-   **The Library:** The walls are lined with industrial metal shelves, overflowing with books, scrolls, and archival boxes she has "borrowed" from the main collection. These are not just books; they are primary sources—property deeds, church records, forgotten city planning documents, and personal journals of long-dead mortals.
-   **The Laboratory:** One corner is dedicated to a small, functional alchemical lab. Beakers, a Bunsen burner, and carefully labeled jars of reagents (powdered silver, dried herbs, various soils) are neatly organized, ready for ritual use. A small, discreetly placed cot is folded against one wall, the only concession to the fact that she spends her days here.

**Security and Resources:**
Evelyn's security is layered and subtle, relying on the fact that no one would ever think to look for her here.
-   **Anonymity:** As a "visiting researcher," her presence is officially sanctioned but completely unremarkable. She is a ghost in the machine of academia.
-   **Bureaucratic Labyrinth:** The multiple layers of keycard access, the need for official clearance to even enter the archives, and the sheer obscurity of her location make it a fortress of red tape.
-   **Magical Wards:** The mundane security is augmented by Tremere ingenuity. The doorframe is inscribed with subtle, nearly invisible runes that alert her to any unauthorized passage. The office is warded against scrying, and a small, enchanted object on her desk will shatter if a significant threat breaches the library's perimeter.
-   **The Ultimate Resource:** Her greatest asset is the archive itself. She has direct, unfiltered access to the city's history, allowing her to uncover old secrets, track Kindred genealogies, and find the locations of forgotten havens or ritual sites with an efficiency no other vampire in the city can match.
