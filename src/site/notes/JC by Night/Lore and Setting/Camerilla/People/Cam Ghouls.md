### Officer Brian O'Connell - Patient Zero

-   **Look:** A classic '90s cop stereotype, but one visibly worn down by a life of moral compromise and supernatural influence. He has a thick mustache, a uniform that always looks slept-in, and a cynical, tired expression that speaks of profound exhaustion. These are the outward signs of a man whose soul is being systematically dissected, a walking testament to [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]]'s ongoing experiment. He looks like he's carrying a weight that is physically and spiritually crushing him.

-   **Role:** **The Inside Man (JCPD).** O'Connell works the night beat Downtown, a crucial cog in the Camarilla's Masquerade. His primary job is to be the first on scene for "weird" calls, logging them as false alarms, gang violence, or anything mundane to prevent mortal authorities from uncovering the supernatural. He's adept at tampering with evidence and redirecting investigations, acting as the first line of defense against mortal scrutiny. For [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]], O'Connell's police work is a vital "field study," providing real-world data on how the Beast's influence can be managed (or mismanaged) in a mortal host.

-   **Connection:** He is blood-bound to "Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]], who treats his corruption and anxiety not as a patient's woes, but as a "case study" in the progression of the vampiric "plague" in a mortal host. O'Connell's growing paranoia, moral compromises, and increasing detachment from humanity are all meticulously documented by Thorne. He is entirely aware that the "doctor" who is supposed to be helping him is, in fact, the source of his decay, a chilling realization that only adds to Thorne's data. His services are on permanent "loan" to [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]], who benefits from O'Connell's ability to keep the mortal world oblivious, though the Prince remains largely unaware of the ghoul's true, deeper purpose for Thorne.

### "Skid" - The Last Believer

-   **Look:** A quintessential '90s skater kid, perpetually stuck at around 17. He sports baggy jeans, a chain wallet, an oversized band t-shirt (likely grunge or punk), and a skateboard he never lets out of his sight. He always has a pair of cheap headphones on, listening to a '90s-era Discman, seemingly oblivious to the world around him, yet keenly observant.

-   **Role:** **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]]'s "Runner" and Street-Level Scout.** Skid is a runaway whom [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]] blood-bound, and he thinks Sal is the coolest, most important guy in the city, desperate for his approval. Sal uses him for crucial daytime errands that Kindred cannot perform: delivering messages to other ghouls, scoping out new businesses, and acting as Sal's eyes and ears among the mortal youth at _The Gilded Cage_. His presence helps Sal maintain a facade of connection to the "scene" he so desperately wants to revive.

-   **Connection:** Blood-bound to [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]]. Skid's unwavering loyalty and naive adoration are Sal's last remaining source of self-worth, making him invaluable to the Toreador's fragile ego. He's also Sal's most reliable source of street-level information, often unknowingly bringing back tidbits that Sal tries to spin into gold or leverage for his desperate schemes to make [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Gilded Cage VIP Room (Sal)\|The Gilded Cage]] popular again. Skid's youthful energy and daytime mobility are a key, if often underestimated, asset in Sal's frantic efforts to maintain his Elysium and avoid oblivion.
