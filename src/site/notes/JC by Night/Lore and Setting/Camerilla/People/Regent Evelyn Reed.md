### Regent Evelyn Reed (Tremere) - The Nexus Seeker (1996)

-   **Look:** Evelyn embodies the crisp, intellectual style of the mid-90s elite, with a practical edge that hints at her hands-on research. She wears impeccably tailored, dark power suits or sophisticated separates (deep greens, burgundies, charcoal) with silk blouses. Her hair is typically a sharp, no-nonsense bob or pinned back, always efficient. Her hands are elegant, often bearing faint smudges from graphite or chalk, and a simple, antique signet ring. She carries a high-quality leather satchel, often containing a well-worn compass or a geological hammer. Her scent is a refined blend of aged paper, expensive ink, and a subtle, almost imperceptible metallic tang, like distant static or ozone, a byproduct of her constant work with raw magical energies.

-   **Role: Regent and Geomantic Engineer.** Evelyn is the formidable Regent of the Tremere Chantry, but her true purpose is revolutionary and deeply strategic for the Pyramid. She has dedicated her unlife to proving a theory: Jersey City is not merely a strategic location, but a **powerful, dormant nexus of magical ley lines**, unparalleled on the East Coast. She sees the city as a vast, untapped magical battery. Her mission, self-appointed and sanctioned by the inner circles of the Pyramid, is to meticulously map these hidden currents, understand their flow, and, most critically, devise the rituals and structures necessary to **exploit and channel their raw magical power** for the exclusive benefit of the Tremere clan and, by extension, the Camarilla. She is the local "occult consultant" for the Prince, a role she finds both insulting to her true ambition and professionally convenient.

-   **Vibe & Motivation:** Evelyn is driven by a singular, immense ambition – to become the architect of a new era of Tremere power, fueled by Jersey City's latent energies. She views the local Kindred political landscape (Prince Durand included) as a mere distraction or, at best, a resource to be managed. Her interactions are precise and transactional. She offers her unparalleled arcane insight to [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]] and the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Camarilla (JC)\|Camarilla (JC)]], but every "service" is a calculated step toward gaining access to another crucial intersection of ley lines, a hidden historical record, or a necessary material for her grand geomantic experiments. She tackles "weird" problems not just to maintain order, but because they are often anomalies or uncontrolled discharges of the very energies she intends to harness. When she speaks, her words are measured, intellectual, and often laced with complex magical theory, revealing glimpses of a mind operating on a far grander, more arcane scale.
    -   She finds [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]]'s treatment of her as a "hired specialist" both demeaning and useful, as it allows her to operate with a degree of autonomy.
    -   [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]]'s "weird" problems are often her opportunities, and she tolerates the Sheriff's bluntness, respecting competence even if the methods are crude.
    -   "Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]]'s clinical approach to vampirism is a different form of scientific pursuit, and Evelyn might see him as a potential, albeit unconventional, source of data or a collaborator on the supernatural underpinnings of the city. She respects his intellect.
    -   She would likely dismiss [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]]'s attempts to pump her for "unique" information for his club, completely missing the true arcane significance of her work, as a tiresome social maneuver.
    -   Her relationship with her childe, [[JC by Night/Players/Lucien Graal (Tony)\|Lucien Graal (Tony)]], is cold and clinical; she views him as a "failed experiment" and a "loose end," treating him like a specimen rather than a progeny.

-   **Haven: The NJCU Geomantic Hub.** Her office within the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/NJCU Archive Office\|NJCU Archive Office]] in 1996 is a dedicated, analog-heavy command center for her geomantic engineering project. It is a fortress of knowledge, secured not by brute force, but by obscurity and institutional bureaucracy.
    -   **The Nexus Map:** The room is dominated by a massive, multi-paneled map of Jersey City, hand-drawn onto large-format drafting paper, spread across a custom-built, sturdy wooden table. It's a masterpiece of occult cartography: overlaid with intricate grids, carefully inked ley lines (some glowing faintly with an internal light), marked with geological fault lines, historical supernatural occurrences, and points of ritualistic significance. Small, meticulously crafted pewter figurines, crystals, and polished stones sit at key intersections, representing potential conduits for her planned manipulations.
    -   **The Historical & Geological Archives:** Surrounding shelves are packed with an eclectic mix: ancient grimoires alongside 19th-century city planning documents, bound volumes of Kindred lineage alongside geological survey reports from the 1920s, and even topographical maps from before significant urban development. A high-quality microfiche reader and a heavy-duty photocopier are essential tools. A secure, off-grid 486 PC with specialized (and probably illegal) mapping software runs in the background, cross-referencing vast datasets.
    -   **The Conduit Workshop:** One corner is a pristine, low-tech workshop. Here, she crafts and tests her tools: dowsing rods of unusual metals, finely cut crystals, intricate chalk diagrams on a portable blackboard, and a series of humming, custom-built resonance meters and psychometric gauges (pre-digital, often involving mercury or delicate needle-based readouts) designed to detect and measure the flow of raw magical energy. The air here literally crackles with latent power, a testament to her constant, dangerous work.
    -   **Security and Resources:** Her haven's security relies on anonymity and bureaucratic labyrinth, augmented by subtle magical wards. Her greatest asset is the archive itself, providing unfiltered access to the city's history, allowing her to uncover old secrets, track Kindred genealogies, and find the locations of forgotten havens or ritual sites with an efficiency no other vampire in the city can match.
