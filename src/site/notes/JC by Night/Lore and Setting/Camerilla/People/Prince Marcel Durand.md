Here's the updated write-up for Prince Marcel Durand, integrating all the established character dynamics and his perspective within Jersey City:

### **Prince Marcel Durand (Ventrue) - The Offended Sovereign**

**My recommendation for Durand:**

- **Generation:** 9th
    
- **Actual Age:** ~160 years old (Embraced circa 1835)
    
- **Apparent Age:** Late 20s / Early 30s### **Blood Potency: 3**

This is the perfect sweet spot for a 9th-gen Ancilla Prince.

- **Blood Surge:** +2 dice (He's noticeably, casually more potent than the coterie).
    
- **Discipline Power Bonus:** +1 die to his rolls when using Disciplines.
    
- **Feeding:** He must feed from resonant mortals to clear Stains, reinforcing his "refined" and picky nature.
    
- **Generational Limit:** He is powerful but still far from a true Elder, which perfectly fits his "insulting test" status.
    

---

### **Disciplines (Ventrue)**

His Disciplines are focused on absolute control, not physical brawling.

#### **Dominate: 4**

This is his primary weapon. His words are law, and his gaze is a command.

- **Powers:** _Compel_, _Mesmerize_ (for giving the coterie their mission), _Forgetful Mind_ (for "cleaning up" messes and maintaining the Masquerade), and **`Submerged Directive`**.
    
- **Why Level 4?** `Submerged Directive` is the ultimate "hands-off" Prince power. He can give the coterie (or any subordinate) a secret, triggered command that they don't even know they have. This perfectly suits his cold, calculating, and hubristic nature. He doesn't need to micromanage; his will is _already_ done.
    

#### **Presence: 3**

This is his "aura" of authority, the reason the room feels cold and oppressive. It's why his "offense" feels so heavy.

- **Powers:** _Awe_, _Dread Gaze_, and **`Entrancement`**.
    
- **Why Level 3?** _Awe_ is his default state, explaining his "old money" allure. _Dread Gaze_ is how he silences Sheriff Petrova with a single look. `Entrancement` creates a powerful, addictive loyalty, which is how he "parades" trophies and keeps figures like Rhiannon (as noted in the coterie file) tragically bound to him.
    

#### **Fortitude: 2**

He's not a warrior, but he is supernaturally durable. His Fortitude is more for his _mind_ than his body.

- **Powers:** _Resilience_ and **`Unswerving Mind`**.
    
- **Why Level 2?** `Unswerving Mind` is the key. It gives him bonus dice to resist any attempt to use Dominate or Presence _on him_. His hubris is backed by a supernaturally strong will. The coterie can't easily lie to him, and other Kindred can't easily manipulate him. He is the one who commands, not the one who is commanded.

#### **Appearance**
Marcel Durand is the picture of cold, detached perfection. His attire is always bespoke, minimalist haute couture from the '90s—think Helmut Lang or early Prada—impeccably tailored and utterly devoid of personal flair. Not a single dark hair is ever out of place. His features are sharp and aristocratic, but his expression is a carefully curated mask of neutral disinterest. He looks less like a ruler and more like a high-end art gallery owner who finds his patrons distasteful. He carries himself with an air of inherent superiority, as if the very act of existing in Jersey City is a personal affront.

#### **Demeanor & Rule**
Durand is "old money" Ventrue, and he rules not through fear, but through disdain. He was "given" the praxis of Jersey City by his sire, an assignment he correctly interpreted as an insulting test of his abilities. As such, he is not paranoid or haunted by his position; he is perpetually *offended* by it. He views the city and nearly all Kindred within it—including his own court—as gutter-rabble, a chaotic mess to be managed with the least possible effort.

He does not micromanage. He delegates with an air of profound boredom and expects absolute perfection. Failure is not met with rage, but with a cold, silent dismissal that is far more terrifying. His court is a place of unnerving quiet and rigid protocol, where courtiers vie for a sliver of his attention, which he grants as sparingly as a king dispensing gold.

#### **Motivation & Flaw**
Durand's primary motivation is to prove his sire wrong. He intends to transform this "backwater" domain into a model of efficiency and profit, not through passion or effort, but through sheer force of will and superior breeding. He wants to succeed so effortlessly that the insult of the assignment is thrown back in his sire's face.

His fatal flaw is a direct result of this worldview: **hubris**. He is so convinced of his own superiority and the inferiority of his surroundings that he cannot perceive genuine threats. He fatally underestimates the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Anarchs (JC)\|Anarchs (JC)]], viewing them as a pack of noisy thugs rather than a disciplined insurgency. He dismisses other rivals as local curiosities, not players in a game he believes only he is qualified to win.

#### **Relationships with the Court & Coterie**

-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]]**: Durand despises Petrova, seeing him as an arrogant, crude Brujah who wouldn't last one night in the refined circles of Manhattan. He views the Sheriff's street-level methods as beneath him, yet grudgingly relies on Petrova to handle the "filth" of the city. He remains oblivious to Petrova's deep-seated contempt and true loyalty to the mortal populace.
-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Regent Evelyn Reed\|Regent Evelyn Reed]]**: He treats Evelyn as a "hired specialist," a useful tool for arcane problems too "weird" for his usual enforcers. He finds her intellectualism tolerable, but her true ambition and the profound significance of her geomantic work are entirely lost on him. He sees her as a means to an end, not a power player in her own right.
-   **"Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]]**: Durand considers Thorne a necessary evil, a peculiar Harpy who somehow extracts useful information from the court's anxieties. He finds Thorne's subtle provocations and "inconvenient truths" mildly irritating but dismisses them as the eccentricities of a Malkavian, unaware that Thorne actively enjoys puncturing his hubris.
-   **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]]**: He declared [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Gilded Cage VIP Room (Sal)\|The Gilded Cage]] an Elysium in a fit of pique, demanding a "civilized" space. He views Sal as a frantic, unreliable manager, barely capable of maintaining the illusion of decorum. He is completely unaware of Sal's desperate attempts to exploit the Elysium status to revive his failing club, seeing only a tiresome sycophant.
-   **[[JC by Night/Players/Rhiannon rere Wright (Lita)\|Rhiannon rere Wright (Lita)]]**: To the court, Rhiannon is his most prized acquisition—a living work of art. She is a symbol of his power, proof that even in this gutter of a city, he can possess something of true grace and purity. He parades her as a trophy, the fragile peacekeeper who lends his brutal reign an air of legitimacy. In private, however, the dynamic shifts. The cold Prince shows a gentleness he affords no one else. He speaks to her softly, holds her arm when a command would suffice, and watches her with a conflicted intensity, as if he doesn't know whether to protect or shatter the one beautiful thing in his domain. He is fully aware of the power the blood bond gives him over her, yet it is her unwavering, uncommanded empathy that seems to fascinate and unnerve him. She is the one exception to his detached rule, a flaw in the sterile white of his existence that he cannot bring himself to correct.
-   **The Coterie (including [[JC by Night/Players/Mia Taylor (Lexi)\|Mia Taylor (Lexi)]], [[JC by Night/Players/Lucien Graal (Tony)\|Lucien Graal (Tony)]], [[JC by Night/Players/Grayson 'The Wall' Walker (Chill)\|Grayson 'The Wall' Walker (Chill)]])**: He views the coterie as his "cleaners," disposable assets tasked with the "filth" he won't touch personally—handling Anarchs, investigating Masquerade breaches, and dealing with anything that requires getting one's hands dirty. He uses them, and their successes are merely proof of his superior delegation, not their competence. He remains largely detached from their personal struggles or ambitions.

#### **Haven**
His haven is [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Durand Tower (Prince)\|Durand Tower (Prince)]], an immaculate penthouse in a Paulus Hook high-rise. It is not a fortress or a home, but a cold, white, minimalist art gallery with floor-to-ceiling bulletproof glass overlooking the Hudson River and the glittering prize of Manhattan. It is the physical manifestation of his soul: beautiful, empty, and untouchable.
