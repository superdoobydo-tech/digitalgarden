# Faction Briefing: The Jersey City Camarilla

**Vibe:** "This is not a domain. It is an insult. Clean it up."

### Overview: The Sinking Ship

The Jersey City Camarilla is a barony under siege, both from within and without. [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]] rules from his fortress on the waterfront, but his grip is built on arrogance, not strength. His mandate is simple: keep the Masquerade and keep his rivals from tearing the city apart. He views this as a chore beneath him. The faction is paranoid, under-staffed, and rotting from the head down. Loyalty is a commodity, and survival is the only true goal.


### Core Territory: The Waterfront

The Camarilla's grip is tightest on the wealthiest parts of the city:

-   **The Waterfront (Paulus Hook & Exchange Place):** This is [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Durand]]'s fortress. The new, sterile high-rises are easy to secure, and the influx of mortal bankers and yuppies provides a wealthy, anonymous feeding ground. The Prince controls the private security of these buildings, giving him a mortal surveillance network.
-   **Downtown:** The old-money core of Jersey City. While its influence is waning, a few key elders maintain havens here, and the JCPD headquarters provides a source of mortal manipulation.

Their influence rapidly fades as you go west. The Heights are [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Anarchs (JC)\|Anarchs (JC)]] territory, and Hoboken is a no-go zone. Liberty State Park is considered neutral ground, a "killing field" for duels or secret meetings.

### Key Figures

1.  **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]] (Ventrue):**
    -   **Look:** Impeccable, cold, and detached. Wears bespoke, minimalist '90s haute couture (think Helmut Lang or early Prada). Not a hair is out of place. He looks more like a high-end art gallery owner than a wise guy.
    -   **Role:** Durand is "old money" Ventrue, "given" Jersey City by his sire as an insulting "test." He's not paranoid or haunted; he's *offended*. He sees Jersey City and its Kindred (including you) as gutter-rabble. He doesn't micromanage; he *delegates* and expects perfection. His weakness isn't fear; it's *hubris*. He fatally underestimates the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Anarch/Anarchs (JC)\|Anarchs (JC)]], viewing them as a pack of noisy thugs rather than a disciplined insurgency. He dismisses other rivals as local curiosities, not real threats.
    -   **Haven:** An immaculate penthouse in a Paulus Hook high-rise, [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Durand Tower (Prince)\|Durand Tower (Prince)]]. It's not a panic room; it's a cold, white, minimalist art gallery with bulletproof glass overlooking the water.
    -   **Relationships:** He despises [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]] but relies on him for the "filth." He treats [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Regent Evelyn Reed\|Regent Evelyn Reed]] as a useful tool, oblivious to her grand ambitions. He finds "Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]] irritating but useful, unaware Thorne enjoys puncturing his hubris. He views [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]] as a tiresome sycophant, unaware of Sal's desperate schemes. He parades [[JC by Night/Players/Rhiannon rere Wright (Lita)\|Rhiannon rere Wright (Lita)]] as a trophy, showing her a rare, conflicted gentleness in private.

2.  **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]] (Brujah):**
    -   **Look:** Pure, working-class Jersey City. He wears a worn, insulated Carhartt jacket, steel-toed work boots, and a permanent scowl of weary vigilance. He carries himself like a union foreman.
    -   **Role:** The *real* power in the city when it comes to violence. A former Anarch, he defected after a Masquerade breach got mortals killed, a failure he took personally. He uses his title as a tool to protect *his* mortal neighborhoods and brutally punish any Kindred who preys on them. He is the city's grim, pragmatic enforcer.
    -   **Haven:** A reinforced auto body shop, [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Petrova's Garage\|Petrova's Garage]], in the warehouse district near the Holland Tunnel. It's a functional armory and a "holding cell" for those who break the Traditions, especially against his community.
    -   **Relationships:** He despises [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]]'s arrogance and detachment. He views "Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]] with suspicion but acknowledges his intelligence. He tolerates [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Regent Evelyn Reed\|Regent Evelyn Reed]] if her "weird" solutions prevent Masquerade breaches. He finds [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]] frivolous and a liability. He watches the coterie with a critical eye, ready to crack down if they step out of line.

3.  **"Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]] (Malkavian):**
    -   **Look:** A disarmingly calm man in simple, clean, almost sterile clothing. His blandness is a professional tool, his eyes holding a disturbing, predatory intelligence. He smells faintly of antiseptic and old paper.
    -   **Role:** The city's unofficial Harpy and Spymaster, but truly a Pathologist of the Soul. He runs a high-end, cash-only mortal therapy practice, [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Montgomery Wellness Center (Doc Thorne)\|Montgomery Wellness Center (Doc Thorne)]], near Montgomery Park. He believes vampirism is a spiritual disease and his "consultations" are clinical studies to extract anxieties and hidden truths.
    -   **Haven:** The [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Montgomery Wellness Center (Doc Thorne)\|Montgomery Wellness Center (Doc Thorne)]] is his meticulously controlled laboratory. Soundproofed rooms for sterile observation, a Faraday cage basement for invasive experiments and disturbing findings. It's neutral ground due to the secrets he holds.
    -   **Relationships:** He finds [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]]'s hubris a fascinating case study, subtly feeding him inconvenient truths. He observes [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]]'s protective rage with detached fascination. [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]]'s desperation is a rich vein of social anxiety for him. He respects [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Regent Evelyn Reed\|Regent Evelyn Reed]]'s intellect as a fellow methodical researcher.

### Street-Level Operatives

1.  **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]] (Toreador):**
    -   **Look:** A short, slick man who insists on wearing gaudy suit vests. He smells of cheap cologne and desperation, a living relic of '90s nightclub glamour.
    -   **Role:** The "manager" of _The Gilded Cage_, a struggling nightclub near Downtown. He has finally convinced [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]] to declare his club "Elysium," seeing it as his last chance to revive his dying club and influence. He is the desperate host of the Camarilla's social hub.
    -   **Haven:** The "VIP" back office of [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/Gilded Cage VIP Room (Sal)\|The Gilded Cage]]. It's a cheap imitation of luxury, with a one-way mirror where he frantically watches the club floor for any sign of success or trouble.
    -   **Relationships:** Terrified of [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]] and desperate to please him. Views [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]]'s bluntness as a threat. Avoids "Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]]'s clinic. Might try to pump [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Regent Evelyn Reed\|Regent Evelyn Reed]] for "exclusive" information, completely missing its arcane significance.

2.  **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Regent Evelyn Reed\|Regent Evelyn Reed]] (Tremere):**
    -   **Look:** Embodies the crisp, intellectual style of the mid-90s elite, with a practical edge. Wears tailored dark suits or separates. Her hands often bear smudges from research. Her scent is a refined blend of aged paper, expensive ink, and a subtle metallic tang.
    -   **Role:** The formidable Regent of the Tremere Chantry. Her true purpose is revolutionary: to map Jersey City's powerful, dormant nexus of magical ley lines and exploit their power for the Tremere and Camarilla. She is the local "occult consultant" for the Prince.
    -   **Haven:** Her office within the [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/Locations/NJCU Archive Office\|NJCU Archive Office]] is a dedicated, analog-heavy command center for her geomantic engineering project, filled with occult maps, historical archives, and custom-built instruments.
    -   **Relationships:** Finds [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]]'s treatment of her demeaning but useful. Tolerates [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Sheriff Nails Petrova]]'s competence. Respects "Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]]'s intellect. Dismisses [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]]'s social maneuvers. Views her childe, [[JC by Night/Players/Lucien Graal (Tony)\|Lucien Graal (Tony)]], as a "failed experiment."


### Notable Ghouls

1.  **[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Cam Ghouls\|Officer Brian O'Connell]] - Patient Zero:**
    -   **Look:** A classic '90s cop stereotype, visibly worn down by moral compromise and supernatural influence. Thick mustache, slept-in uniform, cynical, tired expression.
    -   **Role:** The Inside Man (JCPD). Works the night beat Downtown, first on scene for "weird" calls, logging them as false alarms or gang violence, and tampering with evidence. He is [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]]'s primary, long-term experiment, a living case study in the progression of the "plague" in a mortal host.
    -   **Connection:** Blood-bound to "Doc" [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Dr. Ari Thorne\|Dr. Ari Thorne]], whose services are on permanent "loan" to [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Prince Marcel Durand\|Prince Marcel Durand]]. He is aware Thorne is the source of his decay.

2.  **"[[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Cam Ghouls\|Skid]]" - The Last Believer:**
    -   **Look:** A quintessential '90s skater kid, perpetually stuck at around 17. Baggy jeans, chain wallet, oversized band t-shirt, skateboard, and Discman headphones.
    -   **Role:** [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]]'s "Runner" and Street-Level Scout. A runaway whom Sal blood-bound. He thinks Sal is the coolest, most important guy in the city and is desperate for his approval. He runs daytime errands, delivers messages, and acts as Sal's eyes and ears among mortal youth at _The Gilded Cage_.
    -   **Connection:** Blood-bound to [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sal The Vest Bianco\|Sal The Vest Bianco]]. His unwavering loyalty and naive adoration are Sal's last remaining source of self-worth.

### The Coterie's Role

You (the player characters: [[JC by Night/Players/Mia Taylor (Lexi)\|Mia Taylor (Lexi)]], [[JC by Night/Players/Lucien Graal (Tony)\|Lucien Graal (Tony)]], [[JC by Night/Players/Grayson 'The Wall' Walker (Chill)\|Grayson 'The Wall' Walker (Chill)]], [[JC by Night/Players/Rhiannon rere Wright (Lita)\|Rhiannon rere Wright (Lita)]]) are Durand's "cleaners." He tasks you with the "filth" he won't touch personally—handling Anarchs, investigating Masquerade breaches, and dealing with anything that requires getting one's hands dirty. Prince Durand *uses* you, which makes you a target for Sheriff [[JC by Night/Lore and Setting/Camerilla/People/Sheriff Nails Petrova\|Petrova]], who sees you as the arrogant Prince's pets.
