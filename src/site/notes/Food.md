### 📅 Upcoming Meals

**🥧 Sweet Potato Shepherd's Pie**

- **Ingredients:** 1 lb Ground Pork, 1 Onion (chopped), 3-4 Sweet Potatoes, 1 packet Taco Seasoning, 1 can Corn (drained), 1 can Black Beans (rinsed), 1 cup Shredded Mexican Cheese, 1/2 cup Veggie Broth, Olive Oil, Butter & Almond Milk (for mashing).
    
- **Instructions:**
    
    1. **Topping:** Peel, cube, and boil sweet potatoes until tender. Drain. Mash with a splash of almond milk, 1 tbsp butter, and salt until smooth.
        
    2. **Filling:** Preheat oven to 400°F. In a skillet, cook ground pork and onion in olive oil until pork is browned. Drain fat.
        
    3. Stir in the taco seasoning and veggie broth. Simmer for 2-3 minutes.
        
    4. Stir in the drained corn and black beans.
        
    5. **Assemble:** Spread the pork and bean mixture evenly in a baking dish. Spoon the mashed sweet potatoes over the top and spread gently.
        
    6. Sprinkle the shredded Mexican cheese over the sweet potatoes.
        
    7. **Bake:** Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the filling is bubbly and the cheese is melted and golden.
        
- **"Stovetop Shepherd's Pie"** using the exact same ingredients. Here's how:
    
    10. **Make the Topping:** Boil and mash your sweet potatoes just like the recipe says. Set the bowl of mashed sweet potatoes aside.
        
    11. **Make the Filling:** In a large skillet, make the filling just as directed: cook the **ground pork** and **onion**, drain the fat, then stir in the **taco seasoning**, **veggie broth**, **corn**, and **black beans**.
        
    12. **Assemble in the Skillet:** Once the filling is hot and bubbling, turn the heat down to low.
        
    13. **Add Topping:** Spoon the mashed sweet potatoes in dollops over the top of the filling _right in the skillet_. Gently spread it out.
        
    14. **The "No-Oven" Trick:** Sprinkle your **shredded Mexican cheese** all over the top. Put a **lid** on the skillet.
        
    15. **Simmer:** Let it cook on low for 5-10 minutes. The trapped heat and steam will melt the cheese perfectly and make sure everything is piping hot.
        

**Crockpot Lemon Herb Tilapia**

- **Ingredients:** Tilapia fillets, 1 Onion, Veggie Broth, Lemon Juice, Olive Oil, Garlic Powder, Italian Seasoning.
    
- **Instructions:** Cook base (onions, broth, lemon juice, seasonings) on LOW for 2-3 hours. Add THAWED fish, place on top of liquid. Cook on LOW for 20-30 more minutes.
    

**🧀 Cheesy Salsa Chicken & Rice Skillet**

- **Ingredients:** 1 lb Chicken Breast, 1 cup White Rice, 1 can Veggie Broth, 1 can Black Beans, 1 can Corn, 1 can Diced Tomatoes, 1 packet Taco Seasoning, Shredded Mexican Cheese, Olive Oil.
    
- **Instructions:** Cook chicken in skillet. Add taco seasoning, uncooked rice, and broth. Boil, then cover and simmer 15 min. Stir in beans, corn, and tomatoes. Cover and cook 5-10 min. Top with cheese to melt.
    

### 🍳 Main Dishes

#### Skillet & Stovetop

**🍚 Sweet & Savory Ground Beef Rice Bowl (⭐ A Favorite)**

- **Ingredients:** 1 lb ground beef, 5 tbsp soy sauce, 3 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp hoisin sauce, 1–2 cups cooked rice, ½ onion, finely chopped.
    
- **Steps:** Sauté onions. Brown the beef. Stir in soy sauce, hoisin, and brown sugar. Simmer 2-3 minutes. Serve over rice.
    

**🌮 Taco Skillet**

- **Ingredients:** 1 lb ground beef or turkey, 1 packet taco seasoning, ¼ cup water, 1 can black beans (15 oz), drained, 1 cup frozen corn, ½ cup salsa, ½ cup shredded cheese.
    
- **Instructions:** Brown meat in skillet. Stir in seasoning and water. Cook 2 min. Add beans, corn, and salsa. Simmer 5–7 min. Top with cheese.
    

#### Crockpot & Slow Cooker Meals

**🧅🍗 French Onion Chicken (⭐ A Favorite)**

- **Ingredients:** 4 chicken breasts, 1 packet onion soup mix, 1 can French onion soup, 1 cup shredded Swiss or mozzarella cheese.
    
- **Instructions:** Place chicken in crock pot, sprinkle with soup mix, and pour soup over. Cook on low for 6 hours. Top with cheese before serving.
    

**🍗 Honey Garlic Chicken**

- **Ingredients:** 4 boneless chicken thighs or breasts, ½ cup soy sauce, ½ cup honey, ¼ cup ketchup, 2–3 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder).
    
- **Instructions:** Mix sauce ingredients, pour over chicken in crock pot. Cook on low for 6 hours. Serve over rice or noodles.
    

**🍍 Sweet & Smoky Pineapple BBQ Pulled Meat**

- **Ingredients:** 2 lbs boneless pork shoulder (best) or chicken thighs/breasts, 1 can (20 oz) crushed pineapple or chunks (with juice), 1 cup BBQ sauce, 1 small onion, 1 tsp smoked paprika.
    
- **Instructions:** Layer onion, add meat, pour pineapple and BBQ sauce over. Cook on low for 6–8 hours (pork) or 3-4 hours (chicken). Shred meat and stir into sauce.
    

**🍯 Honey Soy Glazed Pork or Chicken**

- **Ingredients:** 1 lb Pork or Chicken Breast, 1/2 cup Soy Sauce, 1/4 cup Honey, 1 tablespoon Hoisin Sauce, 1 teaspoon Ground Ginger, 1 tablespoon Garlic Powder.
    
- **Instructions:** Place meat in crockpot. Whisk sauce ingredients and pour over. Cook on LOW.
    
- **⭐ CRITICAL NOTE ON COOK TIME:**
    
    - **For Pork Shoulder/Butt (fatty cuts):** Cook LOW for 6-8 hours.
        
    - **For Pork Loin/Chops or Chicken Breast (lean cuts):** Cook LOW for 3-4 hours ONLY. These cuts will become dry and tough if cooked longer.
        

**Easy Crockpot Chili**

- **Ingredients:** 1 lb Ground Beef, 1 Onion, 2 cans Diced Tomatoes, 2-3 cans Black Beans, 1-2 cans Corn, 1 can Veggie Broth, 2-3 tbsp Chili Powder, 1 tbsp Garlic Powder.
    
- **Instructions:** Brown beef and onion. Add all ingredients to crockpot. Stir. Cook on LOW for 6-8 hours.
    

**Simple BBQ Pulled Pork**

- **Ingredients:** 1 lb Pork (Shoulder/Butt is best), 1 Onion, 1 cup BBQ Sauce, 1/4 cup Brown Sugar, 1 tbsp Garlic Powder.
    
- **Instructions:** Place onions on bottom, add pork. Mix sauce, sugar, and garlic powder; pour over. Cook on LOW for 7-8 hours. Shred.
    

**Italian Chicken for Pasta**

- **Ingredients:** 1 lb Chicken Breast, 2 jars Pasta Sauce, 1 tbsp Italian Seasoning, 1 tbsp Garlic Powder.
    
- **Instructions:** Place chicken in crockpot. Pour sauce over. Add seasonings. Cook on LOW for 4-6 hours. Shred. Serve over pasta.
    

**Crockpot Taco Meat (Bowls or Tacos)**

- **Ingredients:** 1 lb Ground Beef or Chicken Breast, 1 packet Taco Seasoning, 1 Onion, 1 can Diced Tomatoes.
    
- **Instructions:** Brown beef. Place beef (or raw chicken) in crockpot. Add onion, seasoning, and tomatoes. Stir. Cook on LOW for 6-7 hours. Shred chicken if used.
    

#### Fish

**Super Simple Garlic & Paprika Baked Tilapia**

- **Ingredients:** Frozen Tilapia fillets, Olive Oil, Garlic Powder, Smoked Paprika.
    
- **Instructions:** Preheat oven to 400°F. Place frozen fillets on a baking sheet. Drizzle with oil, sprinkle with seasonings. Bake 15-20 minutes.
    

**Blackened Tilapia Taco Bowls**

- **Ingredients:** Tilapia (thawed), Taco Seasoning, Olive Oil, White Rice, Black Beans, Corn, Mexican Cheese.
    
- **Instructions:** Pat fish dry. Coat with taco seasoning. Heat oil in skillet. Cook 3-4 min per side. Flake fish. Serve over rice with beans, corn, and cheese.
    

**Honey Soy Glazed Tilapia (Baked)**

- **Ingredients:** Tilapia (thawed), Soy Sauce, Honey, Ground Ginger, Garlic Powder.
    
- **Instructions:** Preheat oven to 400°F. Pat fish dry. Whisk 3 tbsp soy, 2 tbsp honey, 1/2 tsp ginger, 1/2 tsp garlic. Brush half on fish. Bake 10-12 min. Brush on remaining glaze.
    

**Easy Ranch-Crusted Baked Tilapia**

- **Ingredients:** Frozen Tilapia, Ranch Powder Seasoning, Olive Oil.
    
- **Instructions:** Preheat oven to 425°F. Mix 2 tbsp ranch powder with 1 tbsp olive oil to make a paste. Spread on frozen fillets. Bake 15-18 min.
    

**Skillet Tilapia with Tomato-Herb Pan Sauce**

- **Ingredients:** Tilapia (thawed), Olive Oil, Garlic Powder, Italian Seasoning, 1 can Diced Tomatoes (drained), 1/2 can Veggie Broth, 1/2 Onion.
    
- **Instructions:** Pat fish dry, season. Sear in skillet 3-4 min per side. Remove fish. Sauté onion. Add tomatoes, broth, seasoning. Simmer 3-4 min. Return fish to pan to warm.
    

**Crockpot BBQ Pineapple Tilapia (For 6 Fillets)**

- **Ingredients:** 6 Tilapia fillets, 1 can Pineapple Chunks, 1 cup BBQ Sauce, 1 Onion, 1 tsp Smoked Paprika.
    
- **Instructions:** Cook sauce base (onion, pineapple, BBQ sauce, paprika) on LOW for 2-3 hours. Pat THAWED fish dry. Add fillets in a single layer on top of hot sauce. Cook on LOW for 20-30 more minutes.
    

#### Pork Chops

**Lighter Crockpot Italian Pork Chops**

- **Ingredients:** 4-6 Pork Chops, 1 Onion, 1 can Diced Tomatoes, 1 cup Veggie Broth, 1 tsp Garlic Powder, 1 tsp Oregano, 1 tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar.
    
- **Instructions:** Layer onions, then chops. Mix sauce ingredients and pour over.
    
- **Cook Time:** Cook on **LOW for 2.5 to 4 hours**. Check at 2.5 hours. Do NOT overcook.
    

### 🥦 Side Dishes

**Quick Black Bean & Corn Salad**

- **Ingredients:** 1 can Black Beans, 1 can Corn, 1/2 Onion (finely chopped), Olive Oil, Garlic Powder.
    
- **Instructions:** Drain and rinse beans and corn. Mix all ingredients in a bowl. Serve cold or room temp.
    

**Garlic & Herb Pasta**

- **Ingredients:** 1/2 box Pasta, Olive Oil, Italian Seasoning, Garlic Powder.
    
- **Instructions:** Cook and drain pasta. Toss with oil and seasonings.
    

**Roasted Honey-Glazed Carrots**

- **Ingredients:** Baby Carrots, Olive Oil, Honey.
    
- **Instructions:** Preheat oven to 400°F. Toss carrots with oil and honey. Roast 20-30 minutes.
    

**Flavorful Rice**

- **Instructions:** Cook 1 cup White Rice in 1 can Veggie Broth (instead of water). Add 1 tsp Garlic Powder or 1 tbsp Onion Soup Mix to the liquid before cooking.
    

**How to Cook Sweet Potatoes in a Crockpot**

- **Instructions:** Scrub 4 Sweet Potatoes. Prick with a fork. Wrap each in aluminum foil. Place in crockpot (no water needed). Cook on LOW for 6-8 hours or HIGH for 3-4 hours.
